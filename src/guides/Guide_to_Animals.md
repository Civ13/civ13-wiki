# Animals, Dogs & Mounts

This page covers working animals: taming and commanding dogs, riding horses, and hitching animals to carriages. For breeding livestock and collecting milk, wool, eggs and meat, see [Ranching](Guide_to_Ranching.md).

## Naming an animal

Right-click an animal next to you and choose **Name**. You can name ordinary animals that still have their default name. Dogs can only be named by their owner or by members of the faction they belong to.

## Dogs

Dogs appear on some maps, either wild or already belonging to a faction. Wild dogs and wolves show **Wild** in their name.

### Taming

1. Get some **fresh meat**. Rotten meat makes the dog like you less.
2. Switch to **Help intent** and click the dog with the meat. It eats the meat and wags its tail.
3. Keep feeding it. After roughly **twenty pieces of meat** the game tells you that the dog sees you as its owner, and "Wild" in its name is replaced by yours.

A tamed dog joins your faction or civilization. Only fresh meat counts; other food is sniffed and ignored.

### Commands

Dogs obey spoken commands. Say the command so the dog can see you (within seven tiles) and **end it with an exclamation mark**, for example `follow!` The message must start with the command word.

| Command | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| `follow!` | Follows the person who gave the order. |
| `stop following!` | Stops following. |
| `guard!` | Attacks people who come into its area. This is the default. |
| `attack!` | Attacks armed enemies. |
| `kill!` | Attacks enemies whether or not they are armed. |
| `be passive!` | Only fights back in self-defense. |
| `patrol!` | Wanders around the area. |
| `stop patrolling!` | Stops patrolling. |
| `prioritize following!` | Keeps following you rather than breaking off to attack. |
| `prioritize attacking!` | Breaks off following to attack enemies. |
| `stop everything!` | Cancels all current orders. |

The dog's owner and members of its faction can give orders. The owner's orders take priority: if the owner has given an order, other faction members cannot contradict it. Anyone else is ignored, and the dog "refuses to listen".

## Riding horses

Horses can be ridden by anyone; they do not need to be tamed first.

- **Mount:** Click and drag your own character onto the horse. It takes a few seconds, and you cannot mount while lying down or prone.
- **Ride:** Move as normal. A ridden horse can trample people in its way.
- **Dismount:** Click the horse with an empty hand.
- **Pull a rider off:** Click someone else's horse with an empty hand. After a few seconds they are knocked to the ground.

If the horse dies, its rider falls off and is knocked down. Keep your horse fed and away from gunfire.

### Saddles

A **leather saddle** (6 leather, Basic Agriculture research) halves the time it takes to mount and to get off. Use it on a horse nobody is riding to fit it. Riding bareback still works at the normal speed. To take tack off, right-click the animal and use **Remove animal tack**; it drops on the ground. Recover tack from a dead animal before its body decays.

## Carriages

A **carriage** is pulled by a team of horses or oxen. It seats a driver and has five more seats for passengers or cargo such as crates and barrels.

- **Build:** 50 softwood from the vehicle parts section once your people have researched **Carts**, with 20 rope in one hand. It takes some crafting skill.
- **Hitch the team:** Drag a horse, cow or bull onto the carriage. A team is up to four animals of one kind, horses or oxen, and they stand in pairs in front of the carriage, so they need room there. Drag a hitched animal off the carriage to untie it.
- **Drive:** Drag yourself onto the carriage to take the driver's bench. You are handed the **reins**.
  - Press **Z** (activate held object) with the reins to start at a walk, and again to trot or canter.
  - Press **C** (secondary activation) to slow down, and again at a walk to stop.
  - The direction keys turn the carriage. It needs room for its new shape and its team to turn, and you must slow to a walk to turn it right round.
- **Passengers and cargo:** Drag a person, a crate or a barrel onto the carriage to load it. Click the carriage with an empty hand to get off, or to take cargo off. You step down beside the carriage.
- **Examine** the carriage to see its team, its best pace and its free seats.

### Pace and load

- A horse pulls twice as hard as an ox. Oxen only walk, one horse can trot, and two or more horses can canter.
- A heavy load slows the team: passengers, cargo and whatever is packed in the crates all count. Overload it and the team cannot move it at all until you unload it or hitch more animals.
- Roads and built floors are quickest. Rough ground, snow and shallow water are slower, and the team slows going uphill and speeds up a little going down.
- The carriage stops when something blocks its path or its team's path. It runs into people who stand in the way. It cannot cross deep water without a bridge.

### Feeding the team

Pulling makes the animals hungry, and more so uphill. A starving animal pulls at half strength. If an animal dies in harness, it is cut loose and the carriage stops. Feed them before long journeys; see [Ranching](Guide_to_Ranching.md).

A **leather animal harness** (4 leather, Basic Agriculture research) halves the extra hunger a horse or cow gets from pulling. Fit it to the animal before hitching it.

### Carriage papers

Right-click a carriage and choose **Vehicle Papers** to register it to your faction, as with other vehicles. Its owners can name it and sign on crew: a master, coachmen, a wheelwright and grooms. A registered carriage appears in **Our vehicles**. A stranger cannot drive it while none of its owners are around. While an owner is around, a stranger can force the reins, which takes a while and warns the crew.

## Hand carts

A **hand cart** is a small wheeled box you pull yourself. Build one from the logistics section:

| Cart | Material | Holds | Research |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden cart | 40 softwood | 20 | Carts |
| Stone cart | 50 stone | 30 | Carts |
| Copper cart | 30 copper | 40 | Carts |
| Bronze cart | 30 bronze | 50 | Wagons & Ships |
| Steel cart | 30 steel | 60 | Automobiles |

- Click it to open or close it and put things inside, like a crate.
- **Ctrl-click** it to pull it along. Its handles turn toward you as you go.
- An empty cart slows you less than dragging a crate. The more you load into it, the slower you walk, so a full cart is heavy going. **Examine** it to see how hard it pulls.
- A cart can be loaded onto a carriage like any other crate.
