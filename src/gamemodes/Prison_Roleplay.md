# Prison Roleplay

The prison scenario on the public server is **Gulag 13**, a Soviet labor camp, found in the **HRP TDM** and **Civilization 13 (Others)** map votes. Prisoners from several nations work, trade and plot their escape, while the NKVD guards keep the camp running and productive. See the [Gulag 13 map page](../maps/Gulag_13.md) for the layout and roles.

## How the round starts

- The **NKVD** get about **4 minutes** to prepare before the round opens.
- **Prisoners** cannot leave the camp area until **30 minutes** into the round.

## Scoring

Scores are checked every **4 minutes**.

| Side | How they score |
| --- | --- |
| **Prisoner factions** (German, Polish, Ukrainian) | Rubles carried by their living prisoners, plus escape points. A prisoner in the escape area earns **+4** for their own nationality, and **+2** for each other prisoner faction, at each check. Each prisoner death costs **−50**. |
| **NKVD guards** | Camp exports. Ore and wood logged in the **camp exports** book become points and rubles for the camp. |

When prisoners are in the escape area, everyone is told *"There are prisoners currently escaping!"*, so guards know to respond.

## Prisoners

1. **Work your assigned job.** The camp runs on the ore and wood prisoners bring in, and a working prisoner gives the guards less reason for trouble.
2. **Keep your rubles.** Money carried by living prisoners counts for your faction, so dying broke is the worst outcome.
3. **Stick with your nationality.** The German, Polish and Ukrainian groups score separately, and alliances between them are a roleplay choice.
4. **Plan escapes.** An escape only scores while you are alive in the escape area, so prepare food, water and medical supplies first.
5. **Obey the siren.** When the alarm sounds, prisoners must **lie down**. Stay down until the alarm is lifted.

## Guards

1. **Run the camp.** Assign work, supervise the mine and the woodcutters, and log exports in the book to earn points.
2. **Count your prisoners.** Escapes score for the prisoners, so react quickly to the escape announcement.
3. **Use the siren** to stop riots and escape attempts, and give prisoners a fair chance to lie down.
4. **Keep it roleplay.** A guard role does not waive the [server rules](../rules/Rules.md). Killing prisoners without reason ruins the round for everyone.

Injuries are common on both sides; see the [Medical guide](../guides/Guide_to_Medical.md).
