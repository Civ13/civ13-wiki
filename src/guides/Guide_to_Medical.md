# Guide to Medical

On round types that have specified doctors or surgeon jobs please only
join these roles if you have had prior experience with Civilization 13
medical on other rounds, as they are limited and many people will be
relying on you to act fast.

## Severe illness

Plague, typhus, malaria and cholera deteriorate while the infected human is actively processed. Symptoms become debilitating after about three minutes and critical after fifteen: pain, drowsiness, coughing and collapses. The illness does not kill on a timer. Untreated, the body fights it off by itself after about half an hour of play, and then resists that illness for a while. Disconnecting pauses this progression. A sick, collapsing patient is still at risk from other injuries, hunger, thirst and the weather, so keep them safe while they recover.

| Illness | Medicine | Complete oral course |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plague, typhus, cholera | Penicillin | 2 units |
| Malaria | Quinine | 10 units |

Continuous appropriate treatment pauses deterioration and cures the illness after three minutes, much faster than waiting it out. Interrupting treatment resets course completion; provide a fresh complete course before the deadline. Flu and zombie infection use separate behavior.

**Healing herbs** are the early alternative. Activate a herb stack in hand while conscious and able to eat. One herb supplies 10 herbal remedy, lasting about three minutes twenty seconds. Take **three herbs**, redosing before the previous remedy runs out, for a continuous **ten-minute course**. Keep a separate herb for planting; a cultivated harvest starts at three herbs, so building a treatment reserve takes multiple harvests. Herbs come from their jungle-bush forage and herb-stack planting route, not random herb seeds.

For renewable penicillin, extract charcoal and ethanol: **1 charcoal + 2 ethanol makes 1 acetone**, then **10 enzyme + 1 acetone makes 5 penicillin**. Wood-derived charcoal avoids finite coal. The other carbon route uses a barrel: one charcoal item adds three carbon units; a beaker does not accept the item directly. Cinchona supplies quinine. See [Chemistry](Guide_to_Chemistry.md) for bench operations.

A grain or flour starter can ferment without existing yeast. Let the culture grow before withdrawing a yeast container if you want to retain it; each container holds 50 enzyme. Distilling 4 beer produces 2 ethanol. The starter jar needs glass and **Pottery Storage**; apothecary equipment follows **Herbalism** and **Apothecary**. The softwood ethanol-distillery recipe uses the legacy Economy & Production threshold of 45 where that research mode applies.

Susceptible healthy humans can catch plague, typhus or cholera near an infected source. Immunity and sterile-mask protection still apply. Malaria and zombie infection do not use this person-to-person route, and exposure does not reset an already infected patient's progression.

## First-Aid

- The first thing you need to do is drag them (quickly hold CTRL then
click, or right-click then select Pull) away from wildlife or people
chasing/shooting them before they get finished off.

- SHIFT+click or right-click Examine at the person to look more closely
at them, you might see where the bleeding is, but you should ask them as
well as they can tell you about anything that is covered up (if they
don't know how you can use
<span style="font-family:Fixedsys;">LOOC</span> chat to tell them that
they need to click on their face icon on the right side of the screen).
If you have a doctor's handbook you can use that on them to get fastest
details for triage.

- Bandage or trauma-kit any bleeding as top priority, and get them
buckled to a bed which will also help them stabilize. Don't take them
any further than the closest bed unless you have to, and tell them to
stay buckled to the bed and not try walking/crawling as moving around
more with broken bones does more damage. Food and water help healing
too, but do not stuff them full of dried meat as the salt in it can
overdose and do more damage to an already near-death person, so fresh
food is better.

- After main bleeds are stopped, the most injured body parts might also
have internal bleeding, which is important to fix as soon as you can,
but you should not operate while they are "incredibly wounded" when you
SHIFT+click or right-click Examine at them and let bed rest and food
make them slightly safer to operate on first as there's a chance to make
mistakes and do damage. If your character does slip up due to lack of
experience in medicine, poor tools or bad luck, examine them, and
depending on how close to death you might need to stop operating,
cauterise the opened (bleeding) wound and then bandage any bleeding you
caused.

- If doctor's handbooks haven't been invented yet you're going to need
to open this person up and check inside each body part (at bottom-right)
that was bleeding with your bare hands like a true blood-covered
witch-doctor/chiurgeon. Anyone can use [proper tools that get invented
later](#surgery), but in dire situations if your character
has had some previous experience in medicine (*Character tab at the
top-right, Medical [skill](Guide_to_Character.md#skills)

- You can advance with
practice or reading scientific stone slates/papyrus/books*) you will be
able to fix internal bleeding with a simple knife to open an incision,
hemp rope to clamp, wood handle to retract the skin, hemp rope again to
tie the bleeding vein inside, and a burning torch to cauterise to close
the incision back up again. You can fix broken bones with a wooden
hammer or bone-setter after fixing veins but this is not advised if they
are heavily wounded as the steps can fail and do damage when your top
priority is getting the internal bleeding stopped first, and there might
still be other body parts that have internal bleeding you haven't
checked yet. If the chest is one of the body parts do that first and
also try to bandage the heart just in case after the first incision, or
if doing separately after fixing internal bleeding, simply cauterise
again afterwards. Then try do the same for the brain which will also
help with blurry eyes. You can RP this in ancient times as healing
salves and ointments being applied, some of which might have been later
forgotten by history.

- Now you have a person that will stay alive as long as they don't move.
The next step is to check for any broken parts missed, as when someone
moves with broken bones it will do them damage unless it is Splinted
(which is only a temporary fix). Moving with broken skulls leads to
blindness, brain damage and pain paralysis that will lead to eventual
death from starvation/thirst, cold snow or rain, hot sandstorms or other
hazards.

- In the absence of a doctor's handbook, to check body parts for broken
bones click the yellow button at the bottom-right to go into Grab mode,
click them, then double-click the Grab icon that appears in your hands
in the bottom-middle of the screen to double it up and turn it into an
"aggressive" grab - then change to green/help mode and click on the
person with the body part you want to check selected at bottom-right,
and the chat box will start telling you what your fingers find there.
Now [read about Surgery here](#surgery), remind them not to
start walking around again straight after while seriously wounded or the
bone will just break again, they need to stay in bed and heal up before
leaving (again, food and water helps with this). This time is a good
opportunity to talk about the causes of the wound or illness and to get
to know your patient.

### Autoinjectors

The modern combat medicine kit carries **autoinjectors**: one-use pens of morphine, adrenaline or clotting agent. They inject someone else in one second instead of three. The clotting agent seals bleeding wounds one after another over the next minute.

### Time since death

Examine a body from the next tile to see roughly how long ago they died. A medic, or anyone with a **medical** skill of 50 or more, also learns whether CPR or a defibrillator could still bring them back, whether their wounds or blood loss must be treated first, or whether it is too late.

### Reviving the recently dead

Someone who died in the last **8 minutes** can be brought back while their body is still there. On green/help intent, click the body with an empty hand to give CPR: each round of chest compressions takes 5 seconds and has a chance to restart the heart. Keep going until it works. While you keep up compressions the clock stops, so a steady rescuer buys time. Uncover both mouths so your rescue breaths also help.

- A higher **medical** skill, or being a medic, improves the odds.
- **Adrenaline** in their blood (a syringe or autoinjector of 5 units or more) makes each round much more likely to work.
- A charged **defibrillator** restarts the heart in one shock.

Some things keep a person dead for good:

- More than 8 minutes have passed, or the body has started to rot.
- The head is gone, or a vital organ (heart or brain) is missing or ruined. Someone who succumbed has a ruined brain.
- There is a lot of poison in their body.
- The player has moved on: they started a new character, gave up their body or left the game.

Lethal wounds and heavy blood loss only have to be treated first: bandage and heal the body, or give blood, then try again. The revived wake up later, weak and near death, with more brain damage the longer they were gone, so keep treating them. They keep their name and what they knew.

### Triage tags

When several people are hurt at once, a medic, or anyone with a **medical** skill of 50 or more, can right-click a person next to them and choose **Triage Tag**. The tag shows on the body, and anyone who examines the person sees it.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Red | Urgent, treat now |
| Yellow | Serious, can wait a little |
| Green | Walking wounded |
| Black | Beyond help or dead |

Choose **Remove the tag** to take it off.

The same people see a small badge over wounded people around them: yellow for hurt, a red drop for bleeding, a flashing red cross for critical or unconscious, and a skull for dead. In team rounds they only see their own side.

### Physical Healing

#### Bleeding

A bleeding person who is dragged along the ground leaves a smear of blood on each tile, which also shows where they were taken.

**Holding a wound:** Pressing a free hand on a bleed slows it a lot. Use
grab intent with an empty hand and click yourself. The hand holds the limb
you are aiming at if it bleeds, otherwise the worst bleed it can reach (a
hand can't hold its own arm, so use the other hand for that). Each hand
holds one bleed, so you can hold two at once: swap hands and click yourself
again. Clicking yourself again with the same hand moves it to another
wound. You can do this while knocked down. The hand lets go when you pick
something up with it, pass out, or the bleeding stops. To hold someone
else's wound, grab them, tighten it to an aggressive grab, then click them
on help intent with the grab in hand.

**Healing Herbs:** Mostly used by primitive peoples, it will not help
stop the bleeding but will also clean the wound, so it is always
preferable. Can be gathered from bushes with a knife.

**Bandages:** Quick and effective, most people should carry some all
times, reduces the pain on most wounds and helps to treat bleeding as
well. A bandaged wound also halves arterial bleeding. Cloth, leather,
gauze and gelatin bandage rolls all work.

**Silver dressings:** Bandages woven with silver thread, crafted from 1
silver with no research. Each one dresses and disinfects a wound like a
trauma kit without the healing, so it is the antiseptic dressing of the
eras before sulfa drugs. They are still bandage rolls, so they can be
tied as tourniquets.

**Splints:** Lash a broken or dislocated limb still. Iron splints need
medical research; **wooden splints** craft from 2 wood with no
research, so every era can splint a fracture. A splinted fracture
[heals on its own](#splinted-fractures-heal) over time.

#### Severed arteries and tourniquets

A sharp hit can sever the artery in a limb, which bleeds very fast.
Use any bandage roll on an arm, leg, hand or foot with a severed artery
to tie it as a **tourniquet** (one bandage, about 3 seconds). Nothing
bleeds on that limb or below it while the tourniquet is on, and
examining the patient shows it.

- Bandage the wound under the tourniquet. A limb artery with a tourniquet
  and a bandaged wound clots closed after about 10 minutes.
- Once the artery has closed, use a bandage on the limb again to loosen
  the tourniquet. While the artery is still cut, the tourniquet stays
  tight.
- Do not leave it on. After 30 minutes the limb throbs with pain, and
  after 60 minutes the limb dies and needs debridement surgery.
- The [internal bleeding surgery](#internal-bleeding) also repairs a
  severed artery and removes the tourniquet.

**Makeshift tourniquets:** In an emergency, rope or crude twine works as
a tourniquet. Use it on help intent on any bleeding arm, leg, hand or
foot, your own too (rope about 4 seconds, twine about 5). A makeshift
tourniquet only slows the bleeding, but together with a hand holding the
wound it stops it. It doesn't last: rope slips loose after about 5
minutes and twine after about 2. Replace it before then by using a
bandage roll on a limb with a cut artery (this ties a proper tourniquet),
or by tying fresh rope or twine.

#### Splinted fractures heal

A broken limb with a splint mends on its own after about 20 minutes;
time spent resting counts double. The splint then
comes off and drops at the patient's feet. Breaking the bone again
resets the progress. Bone surgery is still the fast way to fix a
fracture.

#### Blood recovery

Lost blood comes back twice as fast while the patient is well fed and
well watered, so give food and clean water after any big bleed.

#### Stimulants, pain relievers and cleansing agents.

**Tea:** Tea heals toxins and helps fight drowsiness and
hallucinations! Cure for flu.

**Charcoal:** Charcoal heals toxins very effectively, slight anti-rad,
but does not help with drowsiness and hallucinations.

**Leech:** Drains toxins and bloods from patients, good alternative to
tea, can end up weakening the patient.

**Coal:** Removes any chemical in the blood.

**Coffee:** Will give you that extra caffeine and increase your speed!

**Cocaine:** A powerful stimulant. Very addictive. 2 times worse
painkiller than opium.

**Peyote Cactus:** Stabilizes critical damage, slight painkiller, and
makes you see many colors. Also using in tribes gamemode for
communicating with gods

**Orange Juice:** Heals suffocation.

**Iron:** Helps replenish blood.

**Alcohol:** Used as a painkiller when there's no opium around. This
includes beer, ale, and rum. Also makes for a great party drink!

**Opium:** Made from poppies and used as an extreme painkiller. Helps a
lot when patients are mostly suffering from pain.

**Willow bark:** Weeping willows grow beside rivers, lakes and swamps in
temperate and taiga lands. Hold a knife on any intent except harm and
click a willow to strip its bark; each tree gives four strips, and the
bark grows back over time. Chew a strip (eat it) for mild, slow pain
relief that also helps fight flu fever. You can also hold the bark and
click an open container with at least 10 units of water to steep it
into a bitter willow bark tea that works the same way. Too much upsets
the stomach.

**Aspirin:** A refined, stronger form of willow bark. It eases pain and
helps fight flu fever. Chemists can make it from willow bark (see the
Guide to Chemistry), and tablet presses turn it into aspirin tablets.

**Potassium Iodide:** Heals radiation. Cure for zombie virus.

**Penicillin:** Cure for typhus, and the strongest antibiotic for
infected wounds.

**Silver:** An antiseptic. On skin it kills germs and it cleans objects,
and taken in it works as a weak antibiotic.

### Wound infections

An infected wound or limb needs an antibiotic. Every era has one:

| Antibiotic | Strength |
| --- | --- |
| Penicillin | Full |
| Prontosil (sulfa) | Three quarters |
| Healing herbs (chewed) | Half; one chewed herb is enough to fight the infection |
| Silver | Weak |

The antibiotic works whether it is in the blood or in the stomach.
Cleaning a wound with a trauma kit, healing herbs, sulfa or a silver
dressing also knocks back a limb infection that has not spread yet, so
clean wounds early.

### Advanced

**Trauma Kit:** Probably the Most useful and effective medical supply,
this specialized kit for trauma wounds will take care of most of the
wounds, stopping pain. Also used in Surgeries to repair Organ Damage.

**Burn Kit:** Mostly used to treat burn wounds, but can also help heal
the area. It treats one burn at a time, like the other medical items.

Very skilled medics close the outer wounds on a limb with a trauma kit
or herbs in one go, but internal bleeding still needs surgery. Any
medical item used on a limb that is cut open on an operating surface
takes part in the surgery instead of treating the skin.

## Continued Care

Without proper care, individuals may end up ill or even worse.

### Diseases

There are currently 5 diseases. Try not to catch them, they will make
your day a whole lot more miserable. Especially in early game, when
advanced medicine doesn't exist.

#### Plague

The rarest and the most dangerous disease. It leaves you weak and
collapsing for a long time unless treated with penicillin.

  - Caught from eating raw mice. Or standing too long near one.
  - Transmits from person to person.
  - It can be spotted by the lumps on your skin and vomiting blood.
  - If you survive, you will get immunity.

#### Malaria

A deadly disease caused by parasites.

  - Transmitted by mosquitoes.
  - Does not transmit from person to person.
  - Will give you very high fevers with shivering and convulsions.
  - Normally survivable without treatment, but it is a long, miserable
    illness.
  - Damage can be greatly reduced by taking quinine (harvested from the
    Chinchona plant).
  - You will not get immunity.

#### Cholera

A disease caused by water-borne bacteria.

  - Caught by drinking straight from puddles or water tiles without
    boiling it first.
  - Cannot be transmitted in any way.
  - Will cause you to convulse and vomit. This will dehydrate you fast\!
  - Vomiting will also cause you to lower the hunger level.
  - Dehydration will kill you without treatment.
  - Treatment is drinking plenty of water until the disease is gone. Any
    type of freshwater will do.
  - **TIP**: You can remove the risk of catching cholera by building a
    well on a puddle. Well water is pure.
  - You will not get immunity.

#### Flu

Just a normal flu.

  - Caught from being dirty.
  - Easily transmitted from person to person. A single person can
    contaminate an entire village!
  - Will very probably not kill you, but can be an annoyance, causing
    blurred vision, blackouts, and fever.
  - Manageable by eating and drinking until full, and resting (buckling
    to a bed).
  - Tea, willow bark tea or aspirin help bring the fever down.
  - You might get immunity after surviving it.

#### Typhus

You've been covered with lice for days and now you feel sick.

  - You might get it from wearing dirty clothes for too long. Watch out for the flea sprites.
  - Throwing up randomly.
  - Slightly transmittable from person to person.
  - Untreated typhus leaves you sick and collapsing for a long time.
  - Treatment is through penicillin and tea.
  - You might get immunity after surviving it.

### Combating Illness

If you are a medical professional or just a beginner you may wish to
learn how to keep people from ending up sick and useless.

  
- **Salt overdose**: Extreme dehydration from drinking sea-water (even
    boiled) or eating too much dried meat 
- you might be able to save
    them by feeding them with a lot of water, and if you have tea that
    will help too.
  
- **Cholera**: Extreme dehydration and vomiting from drinking puddle/
    river water (or a Well with fecies near it) that hasn't had the
    germs cooked out of it. Give them lots of clean water and cooked
    food.
  
- **Food poisoning**: Cooked food, clean water and healing tea.
  
- **Flu:** Causes victims to suffer from weakness or even fever,
    fevers will worsen depending on certain factors, weather and
    fullness. The flu can be cured, it can also cause people to become
    immune to it if they are sick enough.
  
- **Plague:** Take Infected individuals away from large groups, burn
    Infected Corpses and burry them far away.

## Surgery

Besides the usual instruments (Scalpel, Hemostat, Retractor, Bone
Setter, Bone Saw, Cautery) some alternative tools can be used, at the cost
of decreased effectiveness or success.

**Scalpel** 
Alternatives: Knife, Bone Knife, Glass Shard

**Hemostat** 
Alternatives: Rope, Torch

**Retractor** 
Alternatives: Crowbar, Wood Handle, Fork

**Bone Setter** 
Alternatives: Wrench, Hammer

**Bone Saw** 
Alternatives: Hatchet, Tribal Hatchet, Bone Knife
Alternatives for Amputation: + Battle Axe, + Machete

**Cautery** 
Alternatives: Cigar, Torch

### Internal Bleeding

- Usual procedure: Scalpel > Retractor > Hemostat > Bonesaw > Retractor> Trauma Kit/Bandages > Retractor > Cautery

- The vein-fixing step also repairs a severed artery and removes any tourniquet on that limb.

- Alternative (example): Knife > Wood handle > Rope/Cable coil > Torch

### Broken Bones

A splinted fracture also heals on its own over time (see [Splinted fractures heal](#splinted-fractures-heal)); surgery is the fast way.

- Usual procedure: Scalpel > Retractor > Hemostat > Bone Setter > Cautery

- Alternative (example): Knife > Wood handle > Wood hammer > Torch

### Damaged Organs

Bruised internal organs and brain damage heal slowly on their own, faster
while resting, and the pain fades as they heal. Dead, missing or removed
organs do not recover, and infection or blood loss stops the healing.
Surgery is still needed for serious organ damage.

- Usual procedure: Scalpel > Retractor > Hemostat > Bone Saw > Retractor > Trauma kit/Bandages > Retractor > Cautery

- Alternative (example): Knife > Wood handle > Rope > Hatchet > Wood Handle > Healing Herbs > Wood Handle > Torch

### Amputation

Use the bone saw or a hatchet on the affected body part. Not to be used
on the head or limbs, for obvious reasons.

## Human bodies and remains

These mechanics describe the merged game source. A running server must include this update before they apply there.

An exposed dead human body starts to rot after **2 natural season changes** and becomes remains after **4**. On maps without an active seasonal cycle, the warning comes after **6 hours** and retirement after **12 hours of running-server time**. The remains then begin a fresh phase with the same warning and disappearance thresholds. Offline time does not count. Cleanup checks at most 32 candidates every 30 seconds, so busy worlds may take longer after a threshold.

The warning leaves time to recover or revive the body. Revival or rejuvenation clears its decay age. Moving a body or remains to a different location resets exposure; a movement action that leaves it in the same place does not. Bodies and remains inside containers or mobs are protected, including morgues, coffins, closets and vehicles. Pulled or thrown bodies and buckled bodies are protected when checked. Protection resets exposure, and remains containing objects are protected too.

When a body becomes remains, its actual gear drops at the body's location. Bags keep their contents, and guns keep their attachments and ammunition. Existing item identity and raid or insurance metadata stay with the items. Clothing removal can also release pockets, belts and IDs. Useful gear does not become abandoned rubbish because its carrier died, and a physical gear drop does not itself return insurance or credit an extraction loadout.

A body stays intact if gear cannot be dropped, an item is anchored, it carries a non-gear passenger or a loose organ outside its own anatomy, or it has more than 256 direct contents. A failed drop, revival or relocation cancels retirement before body deletion; gear already released may remain on the ground.

Saved corpses stay dead with their damage, nested inventory and accumulated exposure. Saved remains retain their phase progress. A restart does not add offline age or revive a saved corpse. Forced admin season changes keep decay age unless **Advance decay age** is explicitly selected.

On zombie maps, a headed corpse outside the Caribbean admin area reanimates as a zombie at retirement instead of leaving bones. Headless corpses leave remains. Static decorative remains do not expire through this process.
