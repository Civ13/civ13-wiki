# Mining & Metalwork

From the first flint to plated steel. The guides run in the order you meet them: finding stone and ore, smelting and forging, then the later ways to get more out of the rock.

<!-- nav-list:Mining & Metalwork -->
- **[Resource Extraction](../guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md)**: Gathering, mining, digging down, blasting and drilling for oil.
- **[Flint](../guides/Guide_to_Flint.md)**: Finding flint and knapping it into the first tools.
- **[Obsidian](../guides/Guide_to_Obsidian.md)**: Volcanic glass: keener than flint, quicker to chip.
- **[Metallurgy](../guides/Guide_to_Metallurgy.md)**: Molds, furnaces, ores, metals and anvils.
- **[Placer Mining](../guides/Guide_to_Placer_Mining.md)**: Washing gold, tin and other metals out of river gravel.
- **[Ore Dressing](../guides/Guide_to_Ore_Dressing.md)**: Crushing and washing rock to free gold and silver.
- **[Mercury](../guides/Guide_to_Mercury.md)**: Roasting cinnabar for quicksilver, and handling it without poisoning yourself.
- **[Amalgamation](../guides/Guide_to_Amalgamation.md)**: Using quicksilver to pull fine gold and silver out of crushed ore.
- **[Industrial Milling](../guides/Guide_to_Industrial_Milling.md)**: Powered machines that wash and amalgamate ore for you.
- **[Quicksilver Crafts](../guides/Guide_to_Quicksilver_Crafts.md)**: Gilding, mirrors, weather instruments and hats.
- **[Electroplating](../guides/Guide_to_Electroplating.md)**: Coating items in metal for value or longer wear.
