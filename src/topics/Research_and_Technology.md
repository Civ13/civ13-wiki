## Epochs

Civ13 uses ten epochs. The date identifies the stage of the game; the names in menus and research announcements sometimes differ.

| Tier | Epoch date | In-game age name |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 5000 B.C. | Stone Age |
| 1 | 313 B.C. | Bronze Age (also Classical Age) |
| 2 | 1013 | Medieval Age (also Middle Ages) |
| 3 | 1713 | Imperial Age |
| 4 | 1873 | Industrial Age |
| 5 | 1903 | Early Modern Age (also WW1) |
| 6 | 1943 | World War II (WW2) |
| 7 | 1969 | Cold War |
| 8 | 2013 | Modern Age |
| 9 | 2026 | Digital Age |

These are game labels, not historical period boundaries. Not every mode offers every epoch as a starting choice, and the list does not show a server's current epoch. See [Books and Scholars](../guides/Guide_to_Books_and_Scholars.md) for progression in persistent worlds, or [Research](../guides/Guide_to_Research.md) for faction research.
