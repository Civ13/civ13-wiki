# Battle Royale

Battle Royale is an every-player-for-themselves round: the last one alive wins. It has its own **Battle Royale** option in the map vote, with Medieval, Imperial, Wild West and Modern versions. A round holds up to 32 players and needs at least 6. See the [Battle Royale map page](../maps/Battleroyale.md) for the layout.

## Round timeline

| Time | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 minutes | Grace period. Gear up and pick a direction. |
| About 2 minutes in | Areas start closing, one at a time, up to five in total. |
| Before each closure | Warnings at 60 and 30 seconds, then a spoken countdown at 30, 20 and 10. |
| 30 minutes | If nobody has won yet, the round ends in a draw. |

When an area closes it fills with walls, and **anyone still inside is crushed**. The warning names the area, so read chat and move as soon as you see it rather than at the last second.

Respawning is instant, but a dead player is out of the running for the win.

## Winning

The last survivor is announced with *"Winner winner chicken dinner!"*, and the other players are ranked by the order they were eliminated, so lasting longer still counts even if you do not win.

## Tips for your first round

1. **Do not fight in the first minutes** unless you have to. Collect a weapon, armor and something to heal with.
2. **Learn the area names** on the [map page](../maps/Battleroyale.md) so the closure warnings mean something to you.
3. **Stay near the middle.** Edge areas tend to close first and leave you a long run.
4. **Treat bleeding fast.** A small wound becomes fatal over a long round; see the [Medical guide](../guides/Guide_to_Medical.md).
5. **Know your weapon.** See [Combat Basics](../guides/Guide_to_Combat.md) and the [Weapons guide](../guides/Guide_to_Weapons.md).

## Hunger Games

Hunger Games is a related mode run by staff as an event; it is not in the normal map vote. Players start on platforms and must wait three minutes before stepping off. Follow the event briefing and see the [Events guide](Events.md).
