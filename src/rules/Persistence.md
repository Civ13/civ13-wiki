# Persistence Server Rules

Persistence is a high-roleplay (HRP) server with a dangerous, lasting world.
These rules govern its roleplay, conflict, metagaming and offline protection.
The [community conduct](Rules.md#community-conduct),
[historical roleplay and discrimination](Rules.md#historical-roleplay-and-discrimination),
[staff accountability](Rules.md#staff-accountability) and
[reporting and appeals](Rules.md#reporting-and-appeals) standards still apply.

The general escalation requirement and blanket metagaming prohibition do not
apply to Persistence. Neither the Nomads escalation and war rules nor its
permission to raid offline homes, including the separate Semi-Pers exceptions,
should be used in place of this page. Other servers retain their own rules.

## High roleplay

Remain in character during play and treat other characters and the world as
real within the setting. Use OOC channels for out-of-character discussion.

## A dangerous, persistent world

Conflict, deception, theft, violence, territorial disputes and destruction are
part of the game. Players may build remote, defensible homes, acquire superior
equipment and become difficult to defeat. Established players and communities
are not required to surrender their advantages to newcomers.

Game mechanics may be used against other players. Administrators should not
prohibit an action merely because it is hostile, unfair or causes an
in-character loss. Persistence does not require the Nomads sequence of verbal
warnings before conflict. This freedom does not override offline protection or
community conduct and safety.

## Offline griefing

Do not intentionally target players or their property while they are offline
and unable to defend themselves. This is the primary restriction on in-game
aggression.

Accidental damage and ordinary world events are not automatically intentional
offline griefing. Administrators should examine the circumstances and available
evidence before taking action. If a situation is unclear, ask staff for
clarification through adminhelp.

## Metagaming

Small amounts of metagaming are permitted but highly discouraged. Whenever
possible, make decisions using information your character learned in game, and
keep out-of-character knowledge from driving the story.

## Conduct and safety

In-character conflict does not excuse out-of-character misconduct.
Inappropriate slurs, spam, distributing computer viruses and similar abusive
or harmful conduct are prohibited. The community's existing conduct and
anti-harassment standards apply in character and out of character.

## Administration

The role of staff is to enforce these boundaries and investigate the worst
behavior, not to police ordinary use of game mechanics or guarantee a fair
outcome. Distinguish a character's actions from a player's out-of-character
conduct. Use the existing reporting and appeal channels linked above.
