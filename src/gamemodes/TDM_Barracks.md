# TDM Barracks

The TDM server opens in the **Barracks**, a training base that is always running. Players practise there, follow the current battle and keep a battle record between rounds. Battles are fought on a separate battle world, and everyone returns to the Barracks when the round ends.

## Joining a battle

1. Connect to the TDM server as usual. You arrive in the Barracks arrival hall.
2. When a battle forms, the Barracks announces it with a join link. Click the link, the **deployment gate** in the arrival hall, or **Join Battle** in the **Barracks** tab. During a round the same link joins late.
3. On the battle world, ready up and pick a job on the usual pregame screen, then read [Team Combat](Team_Combat.md) for the round itself.
4. When the round ends, the battle world shows its end screen and runs the map vote. After that it sends everyone back to the Barracks.

To leave a battle early, use **Return to Barracks** in the **OOC** tab. Turn on **Auto-join Battle** in the **Barracks** tab (or click the deployment gate) if you want to be sent to every new battle as it forms.

## Following the battle

The **battle status board** in the war room, and **Battle Status** in the **Barracks** tab, show the live battle: the map, phase, round clock, players alive on each side, the win condition and the score. They also list recent battles.

## Battle records

Every battle you fight counts towards your record: rounds, wins, losses and draws, kills and deaths, your best single battle and your time in battle. A kill counts for the last player who hurt the victim, and team kills never count. A round with no winner is a draw.

- **My Battle Record** (Barracks tab), or the **service record** in the hall of fame, shows your totals and your last 10 battles.
- **Leaderboard** (Barracks tab), or the **leaderboard** in the hall of fame, ranks the most kills, most wins, best kill ratio (5 or more battles), best single battle and most time in battle.

## Practising

Inside the Barracks buildings nobody can be hurt. The ranges, yards, pits and arenas are live, and respawn is immediate.

| Area | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Armory | Weapon crates and racks by era, and kit lockers with practice kits for 1713, 1873, 1943, 1969 and 2013 battles. |
| Rifle range | Scored targets from 10 to 60 tiles out, with distance signs. |
| Pistol range | Four lanes for sidearm practice. |
| Grenade and mortar pit | Dummies and crates that come back by themselves. |
| Obstacle course | A timed run from the start pad to the finish pad over trenches, sandbags, mud, wire and barriers. |
| Melee yard and gym | Close-combat practice and [exercise](../guides/Guide_to_Exercise.md). |
| Skirmish arena | Team fights with red and blue ready rooms and a flag. |
| Duelling ring | One-on-one fights with stands for spectators. |
| Medical training bay | [Medical](../guides/Guide_to_Medical.md) practice. |
| Training rooms console | Guided practice rooms for new players, in the arrival hall. |

The mess hall and the tavern are there for waiting between rounds.

### Football

The football pitch is at the south end of the Barracks. Take boots and a jersey from the Red or Blue pitch locker; the locker also puts you on that side for passes and tackles. Goals are announced, the ball returns to the centre spot, and the **pitch scoreboard** keeps the Red-Blue score. The controls are the same as in [Football](Football.md).

## The annex: driving and flying

The stairs in the arrival hall lead up to the annex.

- **Race circuit.** Click a **motor pool console** in the pit garages to have a fuelled car built in the empty bay beside it. It also hands you the car's key: use the key on a car door to unlock it, then climb into the driver's seat. Each bay has its own model, and a bay must be empty and off its short cooldown before it builds again. The circuit has a start and finish line, a drag strip and a gravel rally stage. Timing lines record your laps, and the **lap boards** show the best times. **Race control** starts countdown races.
- **Airfield.** Click a **hangar console** to roll a fuelled light plane onto the apron. Roll down the 41-tile runway at full throttle and the plane takes off by itself into the sky above, then fly the seven numbered **air gates** in order, in low flight, for a timed sky lap. Airspace markers show the edge of the area, so turn back when you reach one. The **flight board** in the control tower shows who is flying and the best sky lap times. See [Aircraft](../guides/Guide_to_Aircraft.md) for the flight controls.

Cars and planes left empty and still for ten minutes are cleared away.

## Faction events

The annex also has the Red and Blue conference rooms, a briefing room and a coordinators' office for faction events. The faction doors open only for members of that faction on the **faction roster**, for event coordinators and for admins. Coordinators and admins keep the roster on the board in the briefing room.

Each faction reads its own **orders board**, and the **event programme** in the briefing room is open to everyone. Coordinators and admins write the boards.
