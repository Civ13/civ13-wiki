# Persistent World Rules

The persistent worlds, such as Gaia, are high-roleplay servers set in a dangerous, lasting world. Conflict, theft, raids, deception and killing are all part of the game, and you may use any game mechanic against other players. Players may build remote, defensible homes and gather better equipment than newcomers; nobody has to give up an advantage they earned.

The persistent worlds have only two rules of their own:

1. **Do not try to remove a player from the game.** No action may be taken with the intention of putting a player out of play. Killing a character is fine. Cutting off someone's limbs and trapping them in a hole while they are offline is not.
2. **Report exploits.** If you discover an exploit, report it to staff promptly and do not use it.

## What this means in practice

- **Metagaming is allowed.**
- **Faction property** can be attacked while any of its defenders are online, even if its owner or other members are offline.
- **Logging out** during a fight or a raid gives no protection.

The [community conduct](Rules.md#community-conduct), [historical roleplay and discrimination](Rules.md#historical-roleplay-and-discrimination), [staff accountability](Rules.md#staff-accountability) and [reporting and appeals](Rules.md#reporting-and-appeals) standards still apply in and out of character, and so do the general rules against hacking and harassment. In-character conflict never excuses out-of-character abuse.

The escalation and war rules for Nomads, and the general metagaming rule, do not apply on the persistent worlds. Other servers keep their own rules. If you are unsure whether something crosses the line, ask staff through adminhelp.
