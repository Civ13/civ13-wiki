# Blacksmith

The Blacksmith turns metal into weapons, armor and tools for the colony on the **Colony** and **Jungle Colony** maps. It is a whitelisted role with two or three slots. Work with the Governor and the merchants to learn what the colony needs before you use up its iron.

## What you start with

| Item | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Hammer | A basic tool, carried on your belt. |
| 25 iron | Enough to build your first iron anvil (20 iron) with some left over. |
| Coins | For buying supplies from the merchants. |

You also start with **very high strength** and a **Crafting skill of 2.5**, well above the **1.7** every anvil requires.

On the colony maps, **only the Blacksmith can use an anvil**. Other colonists get "Your job is not permitted to use this anvil on this map. Ask a blacksmith." That makes you the only source of forged weapons and armor, so keep the colony informed about what you can make.

## Setting up a smithy

1. Pick a spot with the Head Architect. Stone floors and walls are a sensible choice around furnaces.
2. Use your iron to build an **iron anvil** (20 iron). Secure or move it with a wrench.
3. Build a **bloomery** (10 clay) to smelt iron ore into sponge iron, and a **kiln** for casting with molds.
4. Leave room to store ore, fuel, finished goods and orders waiting for collection.

The [Metallurgy guide](guides/Guide_to_Metallurgy.md) explains each furnace and casting with molds.

## Turning ore into goods

1. Get iron ore from miners or the merchants.
2. Smelt it in the **bloomery** to get sponge iron.
3. Work the sponge iron on the anvil to get **iron ingots**. Each unit takes about a second and a half.
4. Click the anvil with the ingots and choose a category.

At the colony's era the iron anvil offers **guns**, **helmets**, **armor**, **other weapons** and several helmet and armor styles. Each recipe shows its iron cost in the menu, and forging takes about one second per unit of iron. You can also assemble flintlock pistols and muskets from parts; see [Flintlock parts assembly](guides/Guide_to_Weapons.md#flintlock-parts-assembly).

Musket balls are not forged on the anvil. Where your map allows them, they are made from iron in the crafting menu's projectiles section.

## Working with the colony

- **Check the written laws** before selling firearms. The Governor may require a permit.
- **Agree on a price first.** Account for materials, fuel and time. An iron cost in the menu is not a fixed price.
- **Take orders in writing.** A paper order list stops two people being promised the same musket.
- **Sell surplus through the merchants.** They can export goods the colony does not need. See the [Merchant guide](Merchant.md).
- **Keep enough iron for the next job.** A full order book is useless if you cannot afford the next batch of ore.
