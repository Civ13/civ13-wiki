# Guide to Traders

Specialist traders are outside dealers who keep a trading post in your settlement. Each dealer takes only certain coins and buys and sells only certain kinds of goods. Use them to turn loot into coin, and coin into supplies you can't make yet.

```admonish info
Trading posts are built on [Civilizations & Nomads](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md) worlds, including Gaia and the other lands.
```

## Getting started

Once your people have researched **Trade & Coinage**, you can craft a trading post from **25 soft wood** in the **economy & production** category. There is one post for each dealer:

| Post | Takes | Pays you in | Deals in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Armourer's post** | silver or gold | gold, then silver | guns, ammunition and ammo parts, armour, helmets, shields, swords, spears |
| **Apothecary's post** | copper or silver | silver, then copper | bandages, gauze, splints, pills, syringes |
| **Scrap dealer's post** | copper only | copper | ore, metal, rope, glass, hand tools |
| **Provisioner's post** | copper or silver | silver, then copper | food, drink, seeds, cloth, leather, pelts, clothing |
| **Jeweller's post** | gold only | gold | gems, pearls, gold valuables, gold nuggets, gold and silver bars, crowns |

A silver coin is worth 10 copper coins, and a gold coin is worth 4 silver.

- **Buying:** click the post to see what the dealer has for sale, then click **Buy**. Pay with coins held in your hands or in your pockets. Only coins the dealer takes count, smaller coins go first, and you get change.
- **Selling:** hit the post with the goods. The dealer names a price, and you choose **Sell** or **Keep**. Unload guns and empty bags and pockets first.
- **Changing coins:** hit the jeweller's post with copper, silver or gold coins and pick the coins you want back. The jeweller keeps a twentieth.

## How it works

- A dealer pays about **half of what an item is worth**, less when it is worn. The armourer won't buy anything below half condition. The apothecary and provisioner won't buy anything below a quarter. The scrap dealer and the jeweller take anything.
- A dealer never pays more than half of what any dealer sells the same item for, so goods can't be traded round in circles for profit.
- Every post of one kind is staffed by the same dealer. All of a dealer's posts in a land share **one stock** and **one purse** for buying. Both refill **every hour**. Once the purse is empty, that dealer buys nothing until the next delivery.
- What a dealer sells changes with the age, and a dealer won't sell anything your people haven't researched yet.
- A [safe house contacts desk](Guide_to_Crafting.md#safe-house-rooms) gets your party 5% better prices from every dealer for each level, both when you buy and when you sell.

Some of the goods on sale, at full price:

| Dealer | Goods | Ages |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Armourer | 10 arrows, spears, leather helmets, medieval helmets, 10 musket balls | early ages |
| Armourer | 20 bullets, 20 rifle casings, 20 pistol casings (30 silver each) | Industrial onward |
| Armourer | WW2 helmet (15 silver), kevlar helmet (30 silver) | by age |
| Apothecary | 5 bandages or 5 gauze, 2 splints, paracetamol, penicillin, trauma kits | by age |
| Scrap dealer | rope, copper, tin, iron, lead, glass, steel (5 at a time), 10 coal, wrenches, crowbars | by age |
| Provisioner | meat, wheat, potato and tea seeds, leather, cloth, bread, MREs | by age |
| Jeweller | a gem (14 gold), a pearl (18 gold) | all ages |

## Tips

- Bring the right coins. The scrap dealer won't touch silver, and the armourer won't touch copper, so ask the jeweller to change them first.
- Sell early in the hour. Once other players have emptied a dealer's purse, you wait for the next delivery.
- Sell worn gear to the scrap dealer or the jeweller, who don't care about condition, if the item is in their line.
