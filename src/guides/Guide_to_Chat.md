# Chat Window and Filter Tabs

The chat window can be split into tabs, so you can follow one kind of chat
without it being buried under everything else.

## The tabs

A row of tabs sits along the top of the chat window:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everything. |
| **IC** | What you hear people say, whisper, sign and emote, plus radio. |
| **Radio** | Radios, telephones and voicepipes. |
| **OOC** | OOC, LOOC, lobby chat and admin PMs. |
| **Dead** | Dead chat, for ghosts and the dead. |
| **Admin** | Admin messages and logs. Only shows up for staff, once there is something to show. |

Click a tab to switch to it. A tab you are not looking at shows a small
number for the new lines it has, and the number clears when you open the
tab. The **All** tab shows every message.

## Making your own tabs

Click **Tabs...** at the right end of the tab row to open the tab settings:

- Type in a tab's name box to rename it.
- Tick or untick the boxes under a tab to choose what it shows. The choices are
  IC speech and emotes; radio, phones and voicepipes; OOC, LOOC and lobby;
  dead chat; admin PMs; admin messages and logs; warnings and combat; and
  other game messages (things like picking up items or examining).
- **Add tab** makes a new tab, and **Remove** deletes one.
- **Reset to default** puts back the standard tabs.
- **Done** closes the settings.

Your tabs are saved on your own computer, so they are still there the next
time you play.

## Repeated messages

When the same message arrives several times in a row, the chat keeps it on one
line and adds a small counter such as **x2** or **x3** after it, counting each
copy. A different message in between starts a new line, so the counter only
ever counts copies that came one after another.

## Chat history

The chat keeps your recent lines when you move to another land, reconnect,
or use **OOC > Reload Chat**. They come back at the top of the chat window,
slightly faded, above a line saying **Earlier chat from your last connection
is above.** Your tabs still filter them as usual.

Links in those older lines no longer work, because they pointed at things in
the land you left. Web links still open. History older than a day is not
brought back, and **OOC > Clear Chat** clears the saved history as well as the
window. The history is kept on your own computer, under your own account.

## Connection warnings

If the server stops answering for about 20 seconds, the chat prints a red
**Connection lost** line. It shows on every tab. The server may have stopped,
or your connection may have dropped. If the server was only busy, a green
**Connection restored** line follows once it answers again. If no restored
line comes, reconnect.

## As a ghost

Ghosts can hear and see more than the living. Three settings in
**Preferences** choose how far that reaches:

- **Ghost ears**: hear all speech, or only speech nearby.
- **Ghost sight**: see every emote on the map, or only emotes nearby.
- **Ghost animal noises**: hear animals from everywhere, or only animals
  near you. Turn this to **Nearby** to keep seeing every player emote without
  the barking, mooing and chirping from across the map. The **Toggle Animal
  Noises** verb in the **Ghost** tab switches it quickly.

## Tips

- A **Combat** tab with only "warnings and combat" ticked makes it easier to
  see who is hitting whom during a fight.
- Each kind of chat keeps its own recent history, so a busy fight will not
  push older OOC or dead chat lines out of their tabs.
- On a server linked to other lands, OOC from another land shows that land's
  name in brackets, such as **[North America]** (see [Travel Between Lands](Guide_to_Travel.md#talking-to-other-lands)).
- Right-click someone next to you and choose **High Five** or **Fist Bump**. They answer by choosing the same on you within five seconds.
- **IC > Emotes** opens a window of emote buttons. Emotes that need a target, such as *point or *hug, are still typed in Say.
- See [Hotkeys](../Hotkeys.md) for the keys that open the chat input bar.
