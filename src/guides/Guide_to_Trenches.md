# Trenches & Fortifications

A trench is a knee-deep ditch that protects the people inside it from distant gunfire. This page covers digging, using and removing trenches, and how they fit with the barricades described in [Construction](Guide_to_Construction.md#fences-barricades-and-barriers).

## Digging a trench

You need a **shovel**: a bone shovel, a shovel with a cast head, or a **trench shovel** made from 2 iron in the Tools section. In research modes your people must have researched **Field Defenses** first; the game tells you if they have not.

1. Stand next to a **dirt** or **sand** tile. On grass or snow, first click the tile with the shovel to remove the grass or snow layer.
2. Switch to **Harm intent** and click the tile with the shovel.
3. Click it again. The first dig leaves a hollow, and the second turns the tile into a trench.

Dirt is quicker to dig than sand. Line up several trench tiles to make a continuous position you can move along.

## Using a trench

- **Getting in** takes half a second. **Climbing out** takes two seconds, so plan your exits before the shooting starts.
- A teammate can **pull you out** of a trench, or help you in, by pulling you across the edge.
- Small animals move in and out freely.
- Lay **wooden planks** across a trench to make a bridge that people can walk over.

### Protection

Trenches change how bullets reach you:

| Your position | Shooter more than three tiles away | Shooter within three tiles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing in a trench | Can only hit your **head**, and often misses. | Normal chance to hit. |
| Prone in a trench | Cannot hit you. | Normal chance to hit. |

The catch: if you fire from a trench while **prone**, your bullet hits the trench wall. Stand up to shoot over the edge, then go prone again to take cover. Grenades and close-range attackers are still dangerous, so cover the ends of your trench.

## Water in trenches

On Nomads, Civilizations and colony maps, a trench dug next to water, or next to another flooded trench, floods. A flooded trench:

- can be drunk from, or used to fill containers, though saltwater is not safe to drink;
- lets boats travel through it, so you can dig a canal from the sea.

For crops, use **irrigation channels** instead. They carry water without blocking movement. See [Farming](Guide_to_Farming.md#watering).

## Filling in a trench

Use a shovel on **dirt** with any intent other than Harm to shovel a **dirt pile**. Use a dirt pile on a trench and confirm to start filling it. Two piles fill a trench tile back to level ground. Sandbags cannot be used for this.

## Pits and climbing

A tunnel shaft you dig with a shovel is an open **pit** with no ladder down. Click it to climb down carefully. Anyone who walks onto it falls one floor, takes some damage and is stunned for a moment.

- **Hide a pit:** use **five leaves** on it and wait three seconds. A covered pit looks like ordinary ground until someone falls in, which tears the cover away.
- **Pulling:** a pull does not carry anyone across a pit. Whoever steps on it falls and the pull is let go.
- **Climbing up:** to get out of a pit or onto a higher floor, face a wall or the rim of a hole with a free hand and use **IC > Climb Up**, or simply click that wall or rim with **grab intent** and an empty hand. After three seconds you climb onto the ledge one floor up, if there is solid, clear ground there to stand on.
- **Ladders:** craft a **wooden ladder** from **4 wood logs** (Tools, needs **Basic Toolmaking**) and use it on a pit to fit it, if there is clear floor at the bottom. Nobody falls into a pit with a ladder. Click the pit to climb down, and click the ladder at the bottom to climb back up. Use grab intent on the pit to pull the ladder out again. A pit with a ladder can't be hidden with leaves.

## Building a defensive line

- Dig the trench first, then build **sandbag** or **dirt barricades** along the side that faces the enemy. Barricades reduce your chance of being hit but can be dismantled. On the [tech tree](Guide_to_Research.md), empty sandbags unlock with **Fortifications**.
- String **barbed wire** in front of the line to slow attackers. Wire cutters remove it.
- Leave a covered route back to your own lines so the wounded can be moved out.

See [Construction](Guide_to_Construction.md#fences-barricades-and-barriers) for barricade recipes, and [Combat](Guide_to_Combat.md#cover) for using cover in a fight.
