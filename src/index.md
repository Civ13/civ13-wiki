# Build a civilization from the Stone Age to the Digital Age

Civilization 13 (formerly 1713) is a free multiplayer game based on Space Station 13 code, played through BYOND, that spans several epochs of human history. It features both roleplay and team-deathmatch modes, with the main one being [Nomads](gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md), in which you bring your civilization from the Stone Age to the Digital Age. See the [full list of epochs](guides/Guide_to_Epochs.md). Visit the [Civ13 website](https://civ13.github.io/) for an overview of the game.

## Start playing

<ol class="wiki-steps">
<li><strong>Install BYOND</strong><p><a href="https://www.byond.com/download/">Download BYOND</a> and install the Windows client. BYOND lists Linux as a hosting platform rather than a game client; macOS is not listed as a supported player platform.</p></li>
<li><strong>Learn the basics</strong><p><a href="rules/Rules.md">Read the server rules</a> and <a href="New_Player_Guide.md">learn how to play</a>. The <a href="Starter_Guide.md">Starter Guide</a> covers your first hour as a nomad, and <a href="guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md">Your First Day</a> walks you through getting a research book, food, water and shelter on persistent worlds such as North and South America.</p></li>
<li><strong>Choose a server</strong><p>These links open BYOND when the client is installed: <a href="byond://civ13.com:1715">Join RP / Nomads</a> or <a href="byond://civ13.com:1714">Join TDM</a>.</p></li>
</ol>

For questions or community help, join the [Civ13 Discord](https://discord.gg/hBEtg4x). To contribute, use the edit links on a page or follow the [wiki contribution guide](Contributing_to_the_Wiki.md). The game source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Civ13/Civ13).

## Our mission

Civ13 brings people together to build civilizations, explore historical settings, and create stories through crafting, cooperation, diplomacy, and conflict.

Our mission is to make that experience deep, creative, and welcoming. New players should be able to ask questions, experienced players should have reasons to share what they know, and contributors should be able to improve the project without accepting personal abuse.

Read our [Mission and Community Standards](Mission_and_Community_Standards.md) for the principles we expect players, contributors, and staff to uphold.
