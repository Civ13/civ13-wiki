# Submarine Commander

![Submarine Commander lobby art](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Civ13/Civ13/refs/heads/master/icons/lobby/subcom13.png)

## Overview

Command a submarine through hostile waters. The whole round is spent aboard the boat: the map is the submarine's interior, and the ocean around it is a 1000x1000 nautical mile grid you navigate from the bridge consoles. Complete the missions relayed over the radio, keep the reactor, air and hull in order, and bring the crew home.

**Objective:** Complete the missions sent over the radio and survive
**Difficulty:** High
**Minimum Players:** No minimum in the vote; a crew of three or more shares the stations

## How to play

Submarine Commander is in the **PvE** group of the map vote, alongside Antarctica and Lights Out. Everything about the boat, from the helm and sonar to flooding and damage control, is in the [Subcom13 guide](../gamemodes/subcom.md).
