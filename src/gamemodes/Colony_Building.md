# Colony Building

Colony Building RP focuses on establishing a settlement with assigned jobs. Colony and Jungle Colony use this mode; other settlement scenarios can have different factions and rules. Check the lobby and briefing before choosing a role.

## Establishing a settlement

Start with drinking water, food and shelter. Agree on where to build before consuming shared materials, then divide gathering, construction, farming and medical work. The [Construction Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Construction.md), [Farming Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Farming.md) and [Survival Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md) explain the ordinary tools for those tasks.

## Roles and relationships

Leaders coordinate priorities; tradespeople and workers provide the supplies that keep the settlement functioning. Ask what is needed before duplicating another player's work. Read the [Colony map guide](../maps/Colony.md) for its roles.

On Colony, natives and colonists are friendly by default. A grace wall becoming passable does not turn a roleplay round into team deathmatch. Follow the briefing and [server rules](../rules/Rules.md) when meeting another faction.

For a settlement event, read the announced setting and your role before joining. Use the [Events Guide](Events.md) to prepare.
