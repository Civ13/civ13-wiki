# Game Modes

Read the lobby's map, mode and round briefing before choosing a role. The map determines the available factions, technology, equipment and objectives. A combat objective does not replace the [server rules](../rules/Rules.md).

| Mode or scenario | What to do first | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nomads and civilizations | Secure food, water and shelter, then agree on settlement and research priorities. | [Civilizations and Nomads](Civilizations_and_Nomads.md) |
| Roleplay scenarios | Read your job and faction briefing; establish relationships and follow the scenario's rules. | [Civilizations and Nomads](Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#what-is-rp-map-mode), [Maps Overview](../maps_overview.md) |
| Team combat | Read your team's objective and the grace-period announcement, then coordinate equipment and movement. | [Team Combat](Team_Combat.md) |
| The Art of the Deal | Contact your company, check its resources and arrange trades. | [The Art of the Deal](The_Art_of_the_Deal.md) |
| Football | Identify your team and position, then enable hotkey mode. | [Football](Football.md) |
| Voyage | Find your officer or assigned station and check the ship's supplies and condition. | [Voyage](Voyage.md) |
| Wizard Boy | Get sorted into a house and follow the student quickstart. | [Quickstart](wizard_boy_quickstart.md), [Wizard Boy](wizard_boy.md) |
| Subcom13 | Assign crew stations and check depth, power and compartment condition. | [Subcom13](subcom.md) |

## Survival scenarios

An island, wasteland or hostile environment can change the priorities of an otherwise familiar mode. Check the [map guide](../maps_overview.md) for the scenario, and use the [Survival Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md) for food, shelter and environmental hazards. Follow the briefing's relationship between factions: a survival scenario is not automatically permission to attack other players.

## Choosing a role

Start with a role whose immediate task you understand. Ask your faction or crew what needs doing before taking scarce equipment. For basic movement, interactions and character creation, read the [New Player Guide](../New_Player_Guide.md) and [Hotkeys](../Hotkeys.md).
