# Team Combat

Team combat scenarios give factions an objective on a particular map. Read the lobby and opening briefing: equipment, victory conditions, round length and grace periods vary between scenarios. Use the [Maps Overview](../maps_overview.md) to find your map's guide.

On the TDM server you wait between rounds in the [TDM Barracks](TDM_Barracks.md), and join each battle from there.

## Your first deployment

1. Check your faction, role and objective before spawning.
2. Coordinate with your team. Identify who will lead, carry supplies and treat casualties. Squad leaders and officers should read [Leading a Squad](../guides/Guide_to_Leadership.md).
3. Equip for your job and check your weapon before leaving the spawn area. Read [Combat Basics](../guides/Guide_to_Combat.md) for the controls, and the [Weapons Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Weapons.md) if you need help loading or operating your weapon.
4. Observe the announced grace period and map boundaries. Wait for the scenario to permit combat.
5. Move with teammates and keep the objective in mind. Report threats and casualties; use the [Medical Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Medical.md) when helping an injured teammate.

## Objectives

| Objective | Team priorities |
| --- | --- |
| Siege | Attackers must take the designated position; defenders must hold it. Read the briefing for the capture area and deadline. |
| Area control | Occupy and defend the announced capture areas. Check capture announcements before moving on. |
| King of the hill | Contest the designated position and maintain control. The map determines how control contributes to victory. |
| Protect a VIP | Keep the designated character alive and follow the scenario's instructions. Assign an escort instead of leaving them alone. |
| Hostage or rescue scenario | Identify the person or group being protected or rescued, and read the required destination or condition before acting. |
| Firefight | Coordinate your team's movement and preserve its fighting strength. Follow the round's stated elimination or score condition. |

When the round ends, the chat lists a few round facts, such as who drew first blood, who had the most kills and which weapon was deadliest.

## Dog tags and the fallen

Right-click a fallen soldier and choose **Take Dog Tags** to take their named tags. Each body has one set. At the end of the round the chat lists every side's fallen, how many of their tags comrades brought home, and how many the enemy took. The same list reads out any medals your officers awarded (see [Medals](../guides/Guide_to_Leadership.md#medals)).

## Friendly fire

If you hit someone on your own side, you are both told who did it, and the round facts count friendly hits. Check your target before you fire.

## Signal smoke

Red, green, yellow and purple **signal smoke grenades** mark a position, a landing zone or a target in thick coloured smoke. Agree on what each colour means with your team before the fight. They call in nothing by themselves. Craft one from **2 tin** in the **Weapons** menu after **Automatic Weapons** research, where crafting is available.

## Mine detector

Armed mines are hard to see. Craft a **mine detector** from **2 electronics** in the **Tools** menu after **Electricity** research, where crafting is available. Switch it on and hold it. It beeps once when an armed mine is within five tiles, twice within three tiles and three times right next to one, and it circles the mines within three tiles for you. Disarm a mine with wirecutters, or dig it up carefully.

## Grace periods and roleplay

A barrier opening or timer ending tells you when an area becomes accessible; it does not by itself establish permission to attack in a roleplay scenario. Follow both the briefing and the [Rules](../rules/Rules.md). Do not assume a timer or capture mechanic from another map applies to the current round.

## Watching as a ghost

After you die, **Toggle Observer HUD** in the Ghost tab shows every soldier's squad chevron, fire team number and health badge while you watch.

## Individual survival rounds

Battle royale uses an individual survival objective rather than the team objectives above. Watch the playable boundary and the round announcements. Check the scenario briefing before treating another player as a teammate.
