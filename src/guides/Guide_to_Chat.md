# Chat Window and Filter Tabs

The chat window can be split into tabs, so you can follow one kind of chat
without it being buried under everything else.

## The tabs

A row of tabs sits along the top of the chat window:

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Everything. |
| **IC** | What you hear people say, whisper, sign and emote, plus radio. |
| **Radio** | Radios, telephones and voicepipes. |
| **OOC** | OOC, LOOC, lobby chat and admin PMs. |
| **Dead** | Dead chat, for ghosts and the dead. |
| **Admin** | Admin messages and logs. Only shows up for staff, once there is something to show. |

Click a tab to switch to it. A tab you are not looking at shows a small
number for the new lines it has, and the number clears when you open the
tab. The **All** tab shows every message.

## Making your own tabs

Click **Tabs...** at the right end of the tab row to open the tab settings:

- Type in a tab's name box to rename it.
- Tick or untick the boxes under a tab to choose what it shows. The choices are
  IC speech and emotes; radio, phones and voicepipes; OOC, LOOC and lobby;
  dead chat; admin PMs; admin messages and logs; warnings and combat; and
  other game messages (things like picking up items or examining).
- **Add tab** makes a new tab, and **Remove** deletes one.
- **Reset to default** puts back the standard tabs.
- **Done** closes the settings.

Your tabs are saved on your own computer, so they are still there the next
time you play.

## Tips

- A **Combat** tab with only "warnings and combat" ticked makes it easier to
  see who is hitting whom during a fight.
- Each kind of chat keeps its own recent history, so a busy fight will not
  push older OOC or dead chat lines out of their tabs.
- On a server linked to other lands, OOC from another land shows that land's
  name in brackets, such as **[Gaia]** (see [Travel Between Lands](Guide_to_Travel.md#talking-to-other-lands)).
- See [Hotkeys](../Hotkeys.md) for the keys that open the chat input bar.
