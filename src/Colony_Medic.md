# Colony Medic

The medic (listed as **Doctor** in the job menu) keeps the colony healthy on the **Colony** and **Jungle Colony** maps. You treat injuries and illness, from routine bandaging to surgery. Keep the [Medical guide](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md) open while you learn.

## What you start with

| Item | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Surgery kit | Scalpel, retractor, hemostat, bone saw, bone setter, cautery and an advanced bruise pack. |
| Doctor's handbook | Diagnoses a patient (see below). Keep it in your pocket. |
| Coins | For buying supplies from the merchants. |

You also start with a **very high Medical skill**, which improves healing items and lets you perform surgery.

## Diagnosing with the handbook

Hold the **doctor's handbook** and click a patient with any intent except Harm. It lists:

- whether they are alive, and their blood type;
- blood loss, general poisoning and malnourishment, graded from minor to critical;
- local injuries to each body part, including limbs that need a proper amputation;
- organ damage and destroyed organs;
- breathing, toxins, hunger and thirst.

Diagnose first, then treat what the handbook shows. It works on corpses too, which helps when someone asks how a colonist died.

## Setting up a clinic

Ask the Governor and the Head Architect for a clinic early. A carpenter can build it if those roles are empty.

- **Beds** for patients waiting or recovering.
- **An operating room** with a table, good light and space to work.
- **Storage** for bandages, kits and herbs, organised so you can find things in an emergency.
- **Water and a way to wash.** See the [Hygiene guide](guides/Guide_to_Hygiene.md); dirty hands and tools spread infection.

Order medical supplies through the merchants, and grow healing herbs as a backup when supplies run short.

## Treating patients

1. **Stop bleeding first.** See [Bleeding](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md#bleeding).
2. **Treat the rest of the damage** with the right kits and medicines.
3. **Operate** on broken bones, internal bleeding, damaged organs and shrapnel. See [Surgery](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md#surgery).
4. **Watch for illness.** Colonies with poor food or water can catch diseases. See [Diseases](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md#diseases).

## Working with the colony

- Agree on any fees before treatment, so patients know what to expect.
- Several medics can join, so split the work: one in the clinic and one out with the guards or work crews.
- Ask colonists to help move patients and fetch supplies when the clinic is busy.
- Stock the shelves before the emergency. Patients rarely make appointments.
