# Events

Civ13 sometimes runs events using its modes and scenarios. An event announcement provides the setting, participation instructions and any additional rules. Check the [Civ13 Discord](https://discord.gg/hBEtg4x) for announcements; this guide is not an event calendar.

## Before joining

Read the announced server, map, roles, start time and rules. Join the specified server and follow the lobby instructions. Ask the organiser if a role or requirement is unclear. An event does not waive the [server rules](../rules/Rules.md).

Modes and maps are selected through votes during ordinary play. Follow the opening briefing or announced match arrangements once the round begins.

## Special scenarios

**Event-city Hunt** puts participants on the run from pursuers. Its opening briefing says not to harm the pursuers; survival through evasion is the objective. This differs from the shipwrecked-pirate Hunt map's Survival RP.

**Player vs NPCs**, including Cursed Island, uses a cooperative scenario objective. Read the opening briefing, organise supplies and roles, and communicate before splitting up. Discover locations and encounters during play.

**Lab breach** gives lab personnel and security a containment objective while prisoners seek escape and control of the announced bridge area. Read the faction briefing and timer announcements; use [Team Combat](Team_Combat.md) for preparation. Do not assume another siege map's victory conditions apply.

## During an event

Follow organiser instructions, watch announcements and report problems without disrupting other participants. Leave hidden discoveries, puzzle solutions and unrevealed encounters for players to find in the round.
