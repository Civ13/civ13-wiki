# Treatments by Ailment

What to look for, what to do at once and what treats each ailment. The
**Available** column says when a society can supply each treatment: "from the
start" means anyone can, from the first epoch, with wild plants, crops or
simple care. Later treatments need the named research. A doctor's handbook
used on a patient shows the same advice, listing only what your society can
supply. See the [Medical](Guide_to_Medical.md) guide for how each treatment
works.

### Wounds and burns

#### Bleeding wounds

- **Signs:** Open or bleeding wounds on a limb; blood on the ground.
- **First aid:** Press and bandage every bleeding wound. A bandage on a limb with a cut artery works as a tourniquet; loosen it within an hour.
- **Limits:** Severe internal bleeding needs surgery; a bandage does not stop it.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressure and a bandage | stops surface bleeding | from the start |
| Yarrow | slows bleeding | from the start |
| Cloth or leather bandages | closes wounds | Herbalism (5000 B.C.) |
| Trauma kits | closes and cleans wounds | Apothecary (1013) |
| Surgery | repairs a cut artery | Field Surgery (313 B.C.) |

#### Burns

- **Signs:** Burned skin on a limb.
- **First aid:** Get away from the heat and keep the burn clean.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comfrey | soothes | from the start |
| Chewed healing herbs | salves and cleans | from the start |
| Burn kits | salves deep burns | Apothecary (1013) |

#### Infected wounds

- **Signs:** A wound that is red, hot and getting worse; fever.
- **First aid:** Clean and dress the wound; keep the patient washed.
- **Limits:** Washing only helps before an infection sets in; after that it needs medicine. An infection left long enough kills the limb.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Washing the wound | halves the germs before infection sets in | from the start |
| Washing with soap | cleans the wound before infection sets in | from the start |
| Rinsing with wine or spirits | cleans the wound before infection sets in | Field Surgery (313 B.C.) |
| Chewed healing herbs | fights the infection | from the start |
| Silver dressings | cleans the wound | Herbalism (5000 B.C.) |
| Trauma kits | cleans the wound | Apothecary (1013) |
| Penicillin | strongest cure | Pharmacology (1903) |


### Fractures and limb loss

#### Broken bones

- **Signs:** A limb that bends wrong, cries of pain when it moves.
- **First aid:** Splint the limb before moving the patient.
- **Limits:** A splinted bone mends in about twenty minutes, twice as fast lying down; surgery mends it at once.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden splints | holds the bone; it mends with time | Herbalism (5000 B.C.) |
| Setting the bone | halves the mending time left | Field Surgery (313 B.C.) |
| Surgery | mends the bone at once | Field Surgery (313 B.C.) |

#### Lost or ruined limbs

- **Signs:** A gored limb or a stump.
- **First aid:** Stop the bleeding first.
- **Limits:** Nothing regrows a lost limb.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surgery | amputates a ruined limb cleanly and fits prosthetics | Field Surgery (313 B.C.) |


### Blood loss and shock

#### Blood loss and shock

- **Signs:** Pale, weak and cold; fainting.
- **First aid:** Stop the bleeding, lay the patient down, keep them warm, fed and drinking.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bed rest | blood comes back faster when fed and watered | from the start |
| Blood transfusion | replaces lost blood | Surgical Theory (1713) |
| Saline glucose drip | replaces lost volume | Surgical Theory (1713) |


### Breathing and oxygen loss

#### Not breathing

- **Signs:** Blue lips, gasping, no breath.
- **First aid:** Get the patient into fresh air. If the heart has stopped, give CPR straight away.
- **Limits:** CPR only works in the first minutes after death.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A bag valve mask | breathes for the patient | Modern Medicine (1969) |
| A defibrillator | restarts a stopped heart | Emergency Medicine (2013) |


### Poisoning and radiation

#### Poisoning

- **Signs:** Vomiting, sweating, weakness after eating, drinking or being bitten.
- **First aid:** Stop the exposure: nothing more from the same food, drink or fumes. Keep the patient drinking and resting.
- **Limits:** A doctor's handbook only shows the signs; a lab test names the poison.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tea | slowly clears poisons | from the start |
| Milk thistle tea | clears poisons | from the start |
| Powdered charcoal | binds poisons in the stomach and blood | Apothecary (1013) |
| A centrifuge blood test | names the poison and how much | Pharmacology (1903) |
| Dimercaprol | draws out mercury | Modern Medicine (1969) |

#### Radiation sickness

- **Signs:** Burns and sickness after time near radioactive material.
- **First aid:** Get away from the source.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Powdered charcoal | draws some out | Apothecary (1013) |
| Potassium iodide | draws it out | found or traded |

### Infections and parasites

#### Influenza

- **Signs:** Sniffing, coughing and sneezing, then a high fever.
- **First aid:** Rest in bed and keep warm. A sterile mask stops it spreading.
- **Limits:** Antibiotics such as penicillin do nothing for flu.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bed rest | halves the illness | from the start |
| Tea | eases the fever | from the start |
| Elderflower tea | eases the fever | from the start |
| Willow bark | eases the fever | from the start |
| Chewed healing herbs | eases the fever | from the start |
| Aspirin | eases the fever | Pharmacology (1903) |

#### Plague

- **Signs:** A deep cough, fever and weakness. Spreads to people nearby and from bodies.
- **First aid:** Keep the patient apart from others and in bed. Bury the dead.
- **Limits:** The body clears it on its own in time, faster in bed.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Chewed healing herbs | a slow full course | from the start |
| Penicillin | a fast full course | Pharmacology (1903) |

#### Typhus

- **Limits:** The body clears it on its own in time, faster in bed.

| Treatment | What it does | Available |

#### Cholera

- **Signs:** Shivering, weakness and fierce thirst after drinking unboiled water.
- **First aid:** Keep the patient drinking boiled water. Only drink boiled water yourself.
- **Limits:** Cholera dries the body out; keep the patient drinking.

- **Signs:** Fever and chills. Passes by blood, not by breath.
- **First aid:** Rest in bed. Never share needles or blood from a sick person.

- **Limits:** Antibiotics such as penicillin do nothing for worms.
