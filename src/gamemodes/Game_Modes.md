# Game Modes

Read the lobby's map, mode and round briefing before choosing a role. The map determines the available factions, technology, equipment and objectives. A combat objective does not replace the [server rules](../rules/Rules.md).

Nomads and TDM host modes and maps selected through votes. For organised rounds, read the [Events Guide](Events.md) and the event announcement before choosing a role.

## Survival scenarios

Use the [Survival Scenarios guide](Survival_Scenarios.md) to distinguish shipwreck, stranded-island, wasteland and competitive survival rounds.

An island, wasteland or hostile environment can change the priorities of an otherwise familiar mode. Check the [map guide](../maps_overview.md) for the scenario, and use the [Survival Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md) for food, shelter and environmental hazards. Follow the briefing's relationship between factions: a survival scenario is not automatically permission to attack other players.

## Choosing a role

Start with a role whose immediate task you understand. Ask your faction or crew what needs doing before taking scarce equipment. For basic movement, interactions and character creation, read the [New Player Guide](../New_Player_Guide.md) and [Hotkeys](../Hotkeys.md).
