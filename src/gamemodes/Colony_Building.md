# Colony Building

Colony Building RP is about founding a settlement with assigned jobs. Colonists arrive by ship, natives already live on the land, and everyone is **friendly by default**. The maps below appear in the **Civilization 13 (Colony & Pioneers)** map vote.

Pioneers Wasteland II, also in this vote, is a zombie survival round; see [Survival Scenarios](Survival_Scenarios.md).

## The first half hour

1. **Pick a role you can stay to play.** Others depend on the Governor, the Head Architect and the craftspeople. The [Colony map guide](../maps/Colony.md) describes each role.
2. **Unload the ship.** On Colony, the ship departs **25 minutes** into the round, after a five-minute warning. Carry the supplies and yourself ashore before then.
3. **Agree on a site** before using shared materials. The Governor and Head Architect decide where things go; ask before building.
4. **Cover the basics:** drinking water, food, shelter and a place to treat the sick. See [Construction](../guides/Guide_to_Construction.md), [Farming](../guides/Guide_to_Farming.md) and [Cooking](../guides/Guide_to_Cooking.md).
5. **Plan for the seasons.** On most of these maps the seasons start changing about 30 minutes in, and winter makes food and warmth harder. See [Winter & Long Rounds](../guides/Guide_to_Survival.md).

Hunger and thirst are on in these rounds, so keep eating and drinking. On Jungle Colony, keep out of the rivers: they hold piranhas.

## How the colony works

- The **Governor** writes the laws and runs the treasury. See the [Governor guide](../Governor.md).
- **Merchants** order supplies from overseas and export goods. See the [Merchant guide](../Merchant.md).
- The **Blacksmith** is the only colonist who can use an anvil. See the [Blacksmith guide](../Blacksmith.md).
- **Doctors** run the clinic. See the [Colony Medic guide](../Colony_Medic.md).
- **Security** enforces the written laws.
- **Carpenters, farmers and colonists** provide the materials and labor everything else depends on.

Ask what is needed before duplicating someone else's work, and report shortages to the Governor early.

## Meeting other factions

The grace wall going down does **not** turn the round into team deathmatch. Natives, colonists and other colonies are friendly unless there is a roleplay reason not to be, and the round start message warns that griefing gets you banned from the mode. Trade, negotiate and follow the [server rules](../rules/Rules.md) when you meet another faction.

For a staff-run settlement event, read the announced setting first; see the [Events guide](Events.md).
