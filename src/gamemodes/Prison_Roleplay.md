# Prison Roleplay

Prison Simulation scenarios give prisoners and guards different responsibilities. Gulag 13, Abashiri, Bagne 13 and River Kwai have their own settings and briefings. Read the selected map's rules, available roles and starting restrictions before joining.

## Prisoners

Find your assigned work, understand your faction and coordinate food, medical care and supplies. Survival, faction progress and escape can be part of the scenario, but the briefing and roleplay rules determine how you pursue them. Do not assume another prison map's escape conditions or grace period applies.

## Guards

Read your role's responsibilities and any access requirements before taking it. Coordinate supervision and supplies with other guards, and follow the server's roleplay rules when dealing with prisoners. A guard role does not waive those rules.

## Starting a round

1. Read the role and faction briefing.
2. Check where your assigned work takes place and who can help you.
3. Read barrier and grace-period announcements before moving between restricted areas.
4. Report injuries and use the [Medical Guide](../guides/Guide_to_Medical.md) for treatment.

The [Gulag 13 guide](../maps/Gulag_13.md) covers that map's setting. For an organised prison scenario, also read the [Events Guide](Events.md).
