# Guide to Companies & the Stock Market

A company lets one or more players own shops together and split the profits. Shares determine who owns a stake and receives dividends. Shares can be given away, sold or inherited. In supported persistent regions, a player house can control who operates a company without owning its shares. Companies exist on Nomads, Civilizations and the other faction-building maps.

## Founding a company

Use **Create Company** in the **Faction** tab. Pick a name and two colours; the colours are painted on every stall the company owns. You start with **100%** of the shares.

Names must be unique, and the name "Global" is reserved.

## House charters

In persistent Nomads and Civilizations regions that support [Player Houses](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#player-houses), you must be a steward of a local house to found a company. After you choose its colours, confirm which house will control the company. You still receive the initial 100% of the shares.

If you are a house steward and hold **100%** of the shares in an existing unchartered company, use **Charter Existing Company** in the **Faction** tab, select the company, and confirm. The charter gives your house control of operations without moving shares, changing balances or restoring a past estate. Companies with multiple shareholders remain shareholder-run; existing companies are never chartered automatically.

A house charter applies in its own region. If a steward dies, that character loses stewardship even if revived. The charter stays with the house, so any other steward can keep the company running even if the deceased character's shares go elsewhere. Appoint a co-steward early; if no steward can act, ask staff about succession. The rules for distributing and inheriting shares do not change.

## Company stalls

Current stewards of a company's controlling house can build, stock and manage its stalls, and configure its fuel pumps. Ordinary house members and shareholders who aren't stewards cannot manage a chartered company's stalls. Unchartered companies still use shareholder access. When building a stall, choose a company you can operate.

| Supply stall | 25 soft wood | Economy & production category |
| Vending machine | 12 steel | |
| Fuel pump | 15 steel | Several styles |

To stock a stall, use an item on it and set its price. A price of 0 gives the item away free. Stacks add to an existing product line. An authorised operator can use a wrench to secure or unsecure a stall. You cannot buy from a company vending stall if you hold shares in the company or operate it as a steward.

Customers put coins (or the stall's fiat currency) into the stall, then pick a product. If you pick a product before putting in enough money, the stall waits; put in the rest and the purchase completes. If the customer belongs to a faction, its **sales tax** is added on top of the price and goes to that faction's treasury. The rest goes into the company's undistributed profits.

## The stock market

Build a **stock market** from 25 soft wood (economy & production category). In tech-tree research mode, it unlocks with **Trade & Coinage**. Click it to open the market:

- **Companies** lists each company with its undistributed profits and how many stalls it owns.
- **Buy Stocks** lists shares for sale. To buy, hold the asking price in your **inactive hand**, in the currency the listing asks for. Change comes back as gold and silver coins.
- **Sell Stocks** puts up some or all of your shares at a price you choose, in standard coins or an active fiat currency. A listing is only an offer: if you give the shares away or die before it sells, the listing is withdrawn. When one of your listings sells, your other listings for that company are withdrawn too.
- **Manage Company** shows the shareholders, and lets any shareholder **Distribute** and **Withdraw** profits. Stewardship alone does not grant either action.

## Profits

Sales build up in the company's undistributed profits. **Distribute** splits that pool between the shareholders by their shares. Each shareholder then uses **Withdraw** to collect their part, paid in gold coins with silver for the change.

When you withdraw, your faction takes its **business tax**. Faction leaders set the sales tax and business tax (0-30% each). A faction with a [treasury strongbox](Guide_to_Money.md#faction-treasury) collects its taxes there as coin. Without one, the leader withdraws them from the stock market's Faction Treasury section.

## Giving shares away

**Transfer Company Stock** in the Faction tab gives some of your shares to a living player within four tiles. Both of you are told how much moved.

## Fire teams

The shareholder with the largest stake can split the company's shareholders into fire teams with **Roster** in the IC tab, the same way a faction leader does (see [Factions](../gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#as-a-leader)).

## When a shareholder dies

A dead shareholder's shares go back to the company. They are shared out among the living shareholders by how much each already owns, and any profits the dead player had not withdrawn return to the company's undistributed profits.

If the dead player was the only living shareholder, their shares go to their faction's leader. If there is no one to take them, the shares go on sale on the stock market.
