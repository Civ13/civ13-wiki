# Ship Damage Control

If the crew is told the hull is holed, **close the watertight doors, patch the leak, then pump the flooded compartment**. With more than one person aboard, have someone bail while the other person makes the repair.

## Find and stop the leak

1. **Check the tiller.** It reports bilge depth, the number of holes, and the water in each watertight compartment if the ship has them. Close the doors between bays so the flooded one doesn't share water with the rest of the ship.
2. **Look at the main deck and in the hold.** They take different repairs:

    | Damage | What to do |
    | --- | --- |
    | Main-deck floor missing, with open water showing | Stand beside the hole on deck. Hold **2 wood** in the active hand and a **hammer** in the other, then use the wood on the hole. The patch closes the hole but can still seep. |
    | **Hold breach** below the waterline | Stand next to the breach in the hold. Hold **2 steel** in the active hand and a **welding tool** in the other, then use the steel on the breach. |
    | Bilge rising but the tiller shows no holes | Start bailing. Check for leaky [seams](Guide_to_Boats.md#caulking), excess [load](Guide_to_Boats.md#load-and-ballast) or heavy weather. |

3. **Drain the affected bay.** Stand there with an empty bucket and use it on a deck floor, or operate its pump. A closed watertight door keeps the next bay dry, so draining the dry side won't help. Check the tiller again before reopening doors.

## The pump is running but water isn't going down

- **Still holed?** A single person with a bucket cannot keep up with an open hole. Patch it first if you're alone.
- **Wrong compartment?** Build pumps in the compartments they'll serve. A pump isn't a pipe that reaches through closed bulkheads.
- **No fuel or power?** Hand-work the lever pump. A steam mine pump needs fuel; a sump pump needs cable power or its battery. See [Bilge Pumps](Guide_to_Boats.md#bilge-pumps) for the types and [Floods and Pumps](Guide_to_Floods.md) for their recipes.
- **Already grounded in the shallows?** Shut every hole and bail below **awash** to refloat. In deep water, don't wait for the entire bilge to read full: a ship can founder when flooded compartments cover more than half the hull.

## Before you sail

Carry an empty bucket, wood and a hammer. If you have a hold, also bring steel and a welding tool. To isolate compartments, run watertight bulkheads all the way across the hull and close their watertight doors. Check at the tiller that the ship actually reports separate compartments, and make sure you can reach each bay's pump.
