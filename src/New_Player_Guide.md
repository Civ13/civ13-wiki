# New Player Guide

## Getting Started

### Read the Rules

Welcome to Civ13. Please take a moment to [read the server rules](rules/Rules.md) before joining a round.

You are welcome to ask questions and make mistakes while learning. Use mentorhelp for gameplay questions and adminhelp for rule concerns or problems. For your first two days, admins and mentors can see that you are new, so they may check in and offer help. Our [Mission and Community Standards](Mission_and_Community_Standards.md) explain the community we want to build together.

### Reporting Bugs

Found something broken? Use **Help > Report bug** in the menu bar (or the **Report Bug** verb in the Help! tab). Give a one-line summary, then describe what happened, how to make it happen again and what should have happened instead. Your BYOND key, the server, map, round and where you were standing are attached for you.

An admin reviews each report. Once approved it becomes an issue on the [Civ13 GitHub](https://github.com/Civ13/Civ13/issues) for the developers, and you get the link in chat if you are online. If it is closed instead, you are told why. You can have up to three reports waiting at a time, a few minutes apart.

### Joining a Server

If you already have the BYOND client installed, skip to the server links below.

1. Download the current stable client from the [official BYOND download page](https://www.byond.com/download/) and install it. BYOND lists Windows as its player platform; its Linux download is for development and hosting, not playing. macOS is not listed as a supported player platform.
2. Open BYOND and search for **Civilization 13**. Select the game and choose **Join**, or use a direct link:
   - [RP / Nomads](byond://civ13.com:1715)
   - [TDM](byond://civ13.com:1714)
3. The game downloads its files and starts in the Lobby. Before joining a round, make sure to read the [Rules](rules/Rules.md).

#### The Civ13 launcher (Windows)

You can also play through **Civ13.exe**, the Civ13 launcher, from the [Civ13 releases page](https://github.com/Civ13/Civ13/releases) (pick the newest "Civ13 Launcher" release). It opens a window with the server list, **Join** and **Refresh** buttons, BYOND sign-in and community links.

- **Refresh** keeps servers it already found in the list and marks the ones that did not answer, instead of removing them.
- When nobody is playing, a server may be asleep. The launcher waits for it to wake up instead of reporting it as unreachable, so give it a moment.
- While you play, the launcher can show "Playing Civ13" on your Discord profile. Turn this off with **Show on Discord**.

### Practice Room

To try the controls before your first round, choose **Practice Room** in the Lobby. You get a private room and a practice body called "Trainee". Nothing you do there affects your real character. One step at a time appears in chat:

1. Walk onto the glowing marker with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
2. Pick up the knife by clicking it with an empty hand.
3. Swap hands with X.
4. Pick up the straw hat and wear it.
5. Open the door in the middle wall and walk through.
6. Choose the red Harm intent (press 4) and hit the practice dummy.
7. Bandage the small cut on your left arm: pick up the gauze, select your left arm on the body doll, then click yourself.
8. Eat the bread slice by clicking yourself with it.
9. Walk onto the exit marker.

When you reach the exit you return to the Lobby. You can leave at any time with **Leave Practice Room** in the IC tab, and **Practice Instructions** repeats the current step. If the world starts saving, the practice room closes and sends you back to the Lobby. If every room is busy, try again in a few minutes.

### In-game tutorial

The first time you join as a character, a short tutorial walks you through the basics on your real character. Each step appears in chat and as a line at the top of the screen; click that line to see the step again. Most steps finish by themselves when you do what they ask:

1. Walk a few tiles with W, A, S and D.
2. Look at your health, food, water and stamina on the HUD.
3. Pick something up.
4. Swap hands.
5. Put an item in a pocket, belt or bag, or wear it.
6. Change your intent.
7. Open the **Crafting Atlas** (IC tab).
8. Eat or drink something.
9. Learn the chat keys and tabs.
10. Find out where to ask for help.

On worlds where research is written into books, one more step covers placing a book on a research bench and studying a chapter.

Use **Got it** or **Skip** in chat to move on. Your progress is saved, so the tutorial picks up where you left off in a later round. To see the current step, stop the tutorial, start it again or **Reset tutorial progress**, open the **Game Menu** with Esc and choose **Tutorial** (also the **Tutorial** verb in the OOC tab). The **Settings** page of character setup shows your tutorial status with a **Reset progress** link.

### Your first round

Use the [Game Modes Overview](gamemodes/Game_Modes.md) to find your scenario's first steps and detailed guide.

Read the lobby's map and mode before choosing a role. In Nomads, start with the [Starter Guide](Starter_Guide.md): secure water, food and shelter before advancing production. On persistent worlds such as Gaia you must study a chapter from a book before you can craft it; [Your First Day](guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md) shows how to get a book and get through the first day. Use [Character & Skills](guides/Guide_to_Character.md) when an interaction says you lack expertise.

Crafting depends on the active map's catalog, era, character skills and research mode. A recipe listed here may be unavailable on your map. For the tech-tree mode, read [Research](guides/Guide_to_Research.md); older modes have separate rules in [Civilizations and Nomads](gamemodes/Civilizations_and_Nomads.md#research).

For later production, see [Chemistry](guides/Guide_to_Chemistry.md), [Power & Batteries](guides/Guide_to_Power.md), and [Crafting](guides/Guide_to_Crafting.md).

Use [Farming](guides/Guide_to_Farming.md) for seeds and irrigation, [Medical](guides/Guide_to_Medical.md#severe-illness) for illness treatment, [Winter & Long Rounds](guides/Guide_to_Survival.md) for cold protection and debris storage, and [Resource Extraction](guides/Guide_to_Resource_Extraction.md) for gathering and powered production.

## TDM / Team Deathmatch Modes

Start with [Combat Basics](guides/Guide_to_Combat.md): it covers intents, aiming at body parts, movement modes, firing, grenades and what to do when you are hit. If you are given a squad leader, officer or radio operator role, read [Leading a Squad](guides/Guide_to_Leadership.md).

Browse [maps and scenarios](maps_overview.md) to see the available battles, and read [Team Combat](gamemodes/Team_Combat.md) for the common objectives.
