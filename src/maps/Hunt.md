# Hunt
After a storm, a pirate shipwreck washes ashore on an island inhabited by natives. This is **Survival RP**: natives and pirates are neutral by default. A grace wall dropping does not authorise an attack on the other faction.

Read the [Survival Scenarios guide](../gamemodes/Survival_Scenarios.md) for preparation. The [event-city Hunt scenario](../gamemodes/Events.md#special-scenarios) is a separate mode with a different briefing.

Factions :

Pirates (Neutral) Natives (Neutral)
