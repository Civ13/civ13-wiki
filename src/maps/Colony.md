# Colony Building RP

Colony Building focuses on civilian roleplay and cooperation. Read the [mode guide](../gamemodes/Colony_Building.md) and [server rules](../rules/Rules.md) before joining. Choose a role you can stay to play, especially when others depend on it.

The difficulty ratings below are rough guidance.
