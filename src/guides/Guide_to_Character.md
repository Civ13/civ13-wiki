# Guide to Character

***"Know thyself."***<br>
*The Delphic maxim*

What is a human being, if not a sum of their physical abilities and mental faculties? The knowledge of one's strengths and weaknesses, the awareness of the workings of the body and the psyche pave the road to the ultimate ideal - steamrolling whatever the world throws at you.

## Character tab

Go to the Character tab on the right panel. There you will see various info about your character, including: 

- stamina level;
- civilization and religion to which you belong, if any; 
- current epoch;
- skill levels (stats).

## Skills

The "standard" maximum level of a skill is 2.5, but it may be higher with the excellent **[mood](../Starter_Guide.md#mood)** bonus and additional **[trait](#traits)** bonuses (for instance, excellent mood and the Gigantism trait provide strength of 4.3125). On the contrary, **bad mood decreases skill levels (for instance, with the horrible mood 2.5 becomes 2.0).**

Skills are trained by various activities and by reading **scientific literature** on respective subjects (skill books - slates, scrolls and books, depending on the epoch). Strength, dexterity and stamina can also be trained deliberately with [exercise equipment](Guide_to_Exercise.md). The literature written by a character with a higher skill level grants a larger skill boost to the reader. **You can only read literature written by others, not by yourself!**

Farming and hunting raise the yield of renewable food. Every 0.01 above 1.0 adds 1% more, so a skill of 1.5 gives half again as much and 2.0 or higher gives double. A skill at or below 1.0 never lowers the yield, and a bad mood lowers the bonus along with the skill.

## Traits

You can select traits on the character setup screen before spawning. Positive (advantageous) traits have to be balanced with negative (disadvantageous)  traits. Selecting a trait gives the description of its effects.

### Playstyle traits

Playstyle traits suit a way of playing. Each one costs nothing because it comes with its own drawback, and you can take only one of them. Most drawbacks only slow how fast a skill trains, so you can still learn everything. Mood effects, food use and the Wanderer's stamina bonus apply on maps where you get hungry.

| Trait | Benefit | Drawback |
| ----- | ------- | -------- |
| Recluse Scholar | Studying and research are 25% faster with nobody else within 5 tiles | 25% slower with people around; mood falls in crowds of 3 or more. Can't be taken with Extroverted |
| Menacing | **Intimidate** (IC tab) sours the mood of someone within 5 tiles who can see you, and wild grazing animals bolt from you | Everyone who examines you sees that you look dangerous; you are a slower tutor |
| Hardened Fighter | Sword and melee skill trains 50% faster; wounds hurt 15% less | Crafting and scholarship train 25% slower |
| Quartermaster | Pulling carts, crates and bodies slows you 40% less; examining a closed crate tells you how many items it holds | Combat skills train 25% slower |
| Green Thumb | Farming trains 50% faster and harvests yield about 10% more | Hunting and combat skills train 25% slower |
| Tinkerer | Crafting x1.1; Engineering scholarship trains 50% faster | Strength x0.9 |
| Wanderer | Stamina recovers 50% faster while you are on the move; you use 10% less food | Mood slowly falls if you stay within 20 tiles of one spot for 45 minutes |
| Rallying Voice | When you shout (end a line with !), friendly people who hear you get a small mood lift, at most once every 2 minutes each | Mood falls when nobody is within 3 tiles. Can't be taken with Introverted |

## Languages

There may be more than one ethnicity on a given map, and each ethnicity speaks its own language. On some maps you can choose who you spawn as, which, basically, equals to choosing a starting language.  On others spawning is random. Furthermore, on some maps you can choose two languages at the start, a "primary" (also determines ethnicity) and a "secondary" one.

**You can learn a new language by listening to it being spoken by others** (first option, takes about 20-30 minutes of continuous listening in total; learning progress is saved). Some languages, for example, German and Dutch, have a degree of mutual similarity, so that a speaker of one language have some initial grasp of the other and can learn it faster. 

As you learn a new language, you will start understanding more and more of it.

**Another option is reading a suitable language book.**  You can write one if you know at least two languages and have 1.35 of the philosophy skill. Reading or writing one needs light: at night or in a dark room, bring a candle, lamp or torch. 

The IC tab has the following commands regarding languages: **Check Default Language**, **Check Known Languages** and **Set Default Language**.

## Combat

You can alter your combat behaviour with the following options:<br>
<img src="../images/hud_combat.png"><br>
from left to right, **tactics**, **mode** and **secondary attack**.

**<u>Tactics</u>**

**Aim** - increases accuracy by 10%

**Charge** - increases melee damage by 10%

**Defend** - increases the chance to parry or dodge by 20%

**Rush** - reduces the melee weapon cooldown by 15%

Click the icon to switch or use the N hotkey in the hotkeys mode.

**<u>Mode</u>**

**Dodge** - focuses on dodging melee attacks rather than parrying. Good when you have a small weapon or fight without a shield. Influenced by the dexterity skill.

**Parry** - focuses on parrying melee attacks rather than dodging. Works better when you use a shield or a larger weapon.

**<u>Secondary attack</u>**

**Bite** - when there are no other options. You bite an arm or hand: aim at one to pick it, otherwise you bite whichever arm or hand is nearest your aim.

**Kick** - when the enemy thinks handcuffing you is enough.

**Jump** - when the enemy thinks you are cornered (maximum jumping distance is 2 tiles).

These are tied to the middle mouse button. Could be switched with the U hotkey in the hotkeys mode.

### Pulling punches

Allows sparring without beating each other to a bloody pulp. On the IC tab, click "Pulling punches" - now you will not harm an opponent when attacking them **unarmed**. Click again to stop pulling punches.
