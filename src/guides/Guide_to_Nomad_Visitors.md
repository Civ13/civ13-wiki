# Nomad Raiders & Caravans

Settlements are sometimes visited by a band of nomads from the wandering clans: either **raiders** who demand tribute, or a **trader caravan** with goods to sell. The staff start these visits as events, one at a time, and only while the people they visit are online. Whatever you do, the clans remember it.

## Raiders

**Warning signs.** You hear distant war horns. The band, two to six warriors led by a chieftain, appears out of sight at the edge of the map nearest your settlement and marches in. Their weapons match the age: spears and swords early on, revolvers from WW2 onward.

**The parley.** When the chieftain comes within about ten tiles of one of you, the band halts and the chieftain demands tribute. You have **90 seconds**.

- **Pay.** Drop items at the chieftain's feet until the demand is met. Tools, weapons, metal, food and anything else with trade value count; weapons and tools count extra. Once the tribute is enough, the raiders pick it up and leave without a fight.
- **Fight.** Attacking any of them, or letting the time run out, starts the assault.

**The assault.** Raiders go after awake people near the settlement, armed ones first. They leave downed people alone. The fight ends after about 15 quiet seconds, or five minutes at most.

**The looting.** After a fight they won, raiders pick up loose items near the settlement, best first, and empty unlocked, unwelded crates and closets. They never take worn clothing or what you hold, they do not touch locked or welded storage, and they do not damage buildings. Whatever they carry off is gone for good.

**Driving them off.** The band flees when its chieftain goes down or half of it falls. A fallen raider drops everything they carried, so you can win your goods back. If nobody is home, or they march for ten minutes without meeting anyone, they turn back.

### Defending against raids

- Keep valuables in locked or welded storage, and lock your doors.
- Keep a few cheap tools, food or coins handy to pay a small tribute.
- Raiders prefer armed targets. Put your fighters in front.

## Trader caravans

A caravan announces itself with **caravan bells** instead of horns. When one of you is near, the trader spreads up to six goods on the ground: leather, pelts, bone, rope, bandages and meat early on, then bronze and cloth, and later iron and an iron knife.

1. **Pay.** Drop items at the trader's feet. Their worth is credited to whoever last handled them.
2. **Buy.** Pick up a good. If your credit covers its price, it is yours.
3. **Don't steal.** Picking up a good you cannot pay for gets a warning. Put it back or pay within 15 seconds, or the guards attack you.

The trader packs up after 12 minutes or when sold out, and hands back any payment you did not spend. Attacking the caravan ends the trade and starts a fight.

## Reputation

All the clans share one opinion of each player, whichever clan visits.

| Deed | Effect on your standing |
| --- | --- |
| Buying from a caravan | +1 |
| Paying a raid's tribute (everyone near the chieftain) | +2 |
| Striking a band that had not drawn weapons (once per visit) | -3 |
| Keeping a stolen good past the warning | -4 |
| Killing a trader or caravan guard | -6 |

Killing raiders who attacked you is self-defence and costs nothing. Your standing is capped at 30 either way and drifts one point back toward zero each real day.

| Standing | Score | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friend | 15 or more | Goods cost 25% less. Raiders ask only half the usual tribute when a friend is present. |
| Trusted | 5 to 14 | Goods cost 10% less. |
| Stranger | -3 to 4 | Normal prices. |
| Distrusted | -4 to -7 | Goods cost 25% more. |
| Wanted | -8 or less | Traders refuse your payment, and raiders who see you attack you on sight, before anyone else. |

## When the server restarts

Visitors are not saved. A restart ends a visit; anything a band was carrying is gone, and goods still laid out by a trader disappear.
