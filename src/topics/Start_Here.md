# Start Here

New to Civ13? Read these in order. The first four get you into a round and through your first night; the rest explain the screen and your character.

- **[Frequently Asked Questions](../FAQ.md)**: Quick answers to the questions players ask most, with links to the full guides.

- **[Your First Day](../guides/Guide_to_Your_First_Day.md)**: A first day on a persistent world: a research book, water, food, shelter and storage.
- **[Hotkeys](../Hotkeys.md)**: Keyboard shortcuts and how to change them.
- **[Customizing Your HUD](../guides/Guide_to_HUD.md)**: What the buttons along the bottom of the screen do and how to rearrange them.
- **[Chat Window & Tabs](../guides/Guide_to_Chat.md)**: Split chat into tabs, filter it and translate other players.
- **[Character & Skills](../guides/Guide_to_Character.md)**: The character tab: skills, traits, birth charts and languages.
- **[Accolades & Characters](../guides/Guide_to_Accolades_and_Characters.md)**: Accolades, extra characters and kin.
- **[Slang](../guides/Guide_to_Slang.md)**: Period words and phrases to roleplay each era.
