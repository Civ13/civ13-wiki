# Obsidian

Obsidian is black volcanic glass. You find it around volcanoes from the very first age, and you can knap it like flint. It makes keener tools than flint does, but they chip sooner and do worse against armour.

## Getting obsidian

- <img src="../images/Obsidian_outcrop.png"> **Outcrops.** Some tiles of cooled lava have black glass showing through. Click one on **Grab intent** to pry loose an **obsidian nodule**. Each outcrop gives three nodules, and then it needs about half an hour before it gives more.
- **Mining near a volcano.** Underground rock walls close to lava or cooled lava sometimes give **rough obsidian**, in addition to any ore. This works on the same level as the lava and on the level below it.
- **Cooling lava.** When fresh lava hardens it sometimes leaves a nodule behind. This happens more often where the lava meets water.

To get nodules from rough obsidian, use a hammerstone on it and you knock off one nodule at a time. A chisel and hammer still cut rough obsidian into blocks for building.

## Knapping

Hold a hammerstone in your active hand. Either a **stone** or an **unsharpened flint** works. Use it on an <img src="../images/Obsidian_nodule.png"> obsidian nodule in your other hand or on the tile next to you, and choose what to make:

| Product | Time | Chance to fail |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Sharp flake | 4 s | Very low |
| Five obsidian arrowheads | 6 s | Moderate |
| Obsidian knife | 8 s | High |
| Obsidian spearhead | 8 s | Moderate |
| Obsidian scraper | 5 s | Low |
| Blade core | 10 s | Moderate |

Obsidian has no grain, so each option fails a little less often than its flint version. Higher **dexterity** makes knapping faster, and higher **crafting** skill makes failures rarer. If a knap fails, the glass shatters into a sharp **obsidian shard**.

The scraper only shows up once you know **Basic Toolmaking**, and the blade core only after **Stone Arms**.

### Prismatic blades

To make <img src="../images/Prismatic_blades.png"> prismatic blades, hold an **antler pick** or a piece of **bone** and use it on an <img src="../images/Obsidian_blade_core.png"> obsidian blade core. This presses off six long, razor-sharp blades. Sometimes the core snaps partway through, and you only get two. A blade cuts and butchers like a small knife.

## Obsidian tools

| Tool | Compared with flint |
| --- | --- |
| <img src="../images/Obsidian_arrowheads.png"> Arrowheads | A nodule gives five instead of three. The arrows hit a little harder but punch through armour only half as well. They fit **arrows** and **atlatl darts**, but not crossbow bolts. |
| <img src="../images/Obsidian_knife.png"> Knife | Cuts harder but wears out in about half as many uses. |
| <img src="../images/Obsidian_spear.png"> Spear | Use an obsidian spearhead on a branch. It hits harder than a flint spear but chips sooner. |
| <img src="../images/Obsidian_scraper.png"> Scraper | Saves an extra piece of hide while skinning, like the flint hide scraper. |

Any obsidian edge in either hand (a knife, scraper, flake or blade) makes **butchering and skinning about a quarter faster**.
